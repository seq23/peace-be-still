# Privacy policy

Draft for the planned Peace Be Still app. This policy must be reconciled with the shipped app and its providers before publication.

## Information used by the app

Your selected settings, saved verses, journal entries and recordings are intended to stay on your device. Deleting the app or its local data may remove them. Chat messages are sent to our service and to AI providers to generate a response; those providers may retain and use what you type to improve their services. Please leave out names and other identifying details. Paid chat memory is a short summary associated with an installation identifier; you can delete it in Settings.

Our service may process an installation identifier, IP address and usage counts to provide chat, enforce limits and protect the service. Apple processes purchases; RevenueCat receives purchase and installation information to manage access. Hosting and AI providers process information needed to operate the service. Their own policies also apply. We do not offer advertising in the app.

Chat is not offered to children under 13. Teens use the teen chat experience. Do not enter someone else's private information into chat.

## Your choices

You can delete local entries and recordings on your device, and delete paid chat memory in Settings. Apple manages subscriptions through its subscription settings. Retention periods, support contact and any additional access or deletion options must be confirmed against the production implementation before this policy is published.
