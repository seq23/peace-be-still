# Privacy policy

Effective: November 20, 2026

Peace Be Still is operated by Spry Labs. Contact info@peacebestillapp.com with privacy questions or requests.

## Information we use

Your settings, saved verses, journal entries and recordings stay on your device. Deleting the app or its local data may remove them.

With your permission, chat messages go through our service to Cloudflare Workers AI, Google Gemini (free tier) and free models reached through OpenRouter. These providers may retain and use what you type to improve their services, and people at those companies may read it. Please leave out names and identifying details. Each chat message is sent with your age group (teen or adult), the space you are in, the chat voice and the language, so the answer fits; none of these is linked to your name, because the app never asks for it. Messages that mention self-harm or abuse are never sent to an AI provider; the app answers with crisis lines instead.

We do not store chat text. It passes through to the provider and is gone from our service. Paid chat memory is a short summary associated with an install identifier. It is kept until you delete it in Settings or 30 days after your paid plan ends. IP addresses and install identifiers are used for rate-limit counters and held no longer than 48 hours. Daily counts are kept as totals without text or identifiers.

Apple processes purchases; RevenueCat receives purchase and installation information to manage access. We have no ads, no ad SDKs and no analytics SDKs. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell or share personal information. Browser Do Not Track signals do not apply because there is no tracking to stop.

## Children

Under 13, the app sends no chat, memory or install id to our service. Birth year stays on the device. Audio files are fetched like any web file: the IP address is used to deliver the file. Purchases go through Apple, with a parent through Family Sharing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child sent us information, write to info@peacebestillapp.com and we will delete it.

## Your choices and changes

Delete local entries and recordings on your device, and paid chat memory in Settings. Apple manages subscriptions through its subscription settings. When this policy changes, we post the new date here and show a notice in the app for material changes.
