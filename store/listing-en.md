Description first 3 lines:
A great calm, every night.
Peace Be Still is one quiet place for the end of the day and the start of the next: whispered sessions to fall asleep to, a word for the morning, and a calm chat to talk things through.
Choose your way in: God's Word, or mindset & imagination. Each one is its own space, and you can switch any time in Settings.

Description body:
Free, and it stays free: the whole Bible to read, and nearly every chapter read aloud. Today's story. One chat a day, up to 5 messages. Your first sleep night, then one a week. A word for each morning. Basic 7-day plans, saved verses, a journal, a streak that never scolds, and cards to share.

A paid plan adds unlimited Bible chat, chat that remembers what you are carrying, a session every night, sessions made for you, every past story, longer plans, declaration topics and offline listening. Unlimited Bible chat. Private for your first 50 messages each day; after that, chat keeps going, just not private. Your private chats are never used to train AI.

Scripture is quoted word for word from the King James Version, and the Reina-Valera 1909 in Spanish.

Chat answers are written by AI and marked AI; what you type goes to free AI providers that may learn from it.

No trial. A paid plan renews monthly or yearly until you cancel. Prices and renewal terms appear before purchase. Cancel any time in Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions.
