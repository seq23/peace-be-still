For ages 13 to 17.

Young Faith gives you a short Bible story every day, a declaration to say out loud and the whole King James Bible to read, with every verse quoted word for word.

Free, and it stays free: the whole Bible to read, and nearly every chapter read aloud. Today's story. One chat a day, up to 5 messages, with every answer marked AI. Your first sleep night, then one a week. A journal and saved verses.

A paid plan adds a session every night, every past story, longer reading plans, declaration topics and offline listening.

Chat answers are written by AI and marked AI. They are not a pastor, a counselor or a person. What you type in free chat goes to a free AI provider, so please do not share names or private details. There are no ads, and the app never asks for your name.

No trial. A paid plan renews monthly or yearly until you cancel. Google Play shows the price and renewal terms before you buy. Cancel any time in the Play Store app: tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
