Full description:
Fall asleep inside the scene you hope for.

Imagination Nights are whispered night sessions for the law of assumption: a short teaching, the scene read back softly, then quiet for sleep. A calm voice, a sleep timer that fades out, and no quiz before you start. Your first night is free, then one a week.

Write your own night (paid, 18 and over): write what you hope for, and AI turns it into one short moment, as if it has already happened, that you can change word for word. The phone's voice reads it softly over a quiet sound as you fall asleep.

Free, and it stays free: one chat a day, up to 5 messages, a word for each morning, a journal, and your first night, then one a week.

A paid plan adds unlimited chat, chat that remembers what you share, a session every night, sessions made for you, Write your own night (18 and over), declaration topics and offline listening. Private for your first 50 messages each day; after that, chat keeps going, just not private. Your private chats are never used to train AI.

Chat answers and moments are written by AI and marked AI; what you type in free chat goes to free AI providers that may learn from it. The voices are made with AI. There are no ads, and the app never asks for your name.

No trial. A paid plan renews monthly or yearly until you cancel. Google Play shows the price and renewal terms before you buy. Cancel any time in the Play Store app: tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
