Hush Harbor · Black Bible Chat & Stories.

Talk it through in a Black church voice, with every verse quoted word for word from the King James Bible. A new Bible story every day, told in that same voice, and a shelf of Bible stories to read in Nights.

At night, whispered Psalms to fall asleep to, with a sleep timer that fades out.

Free, and it stays free: the whole Bible to read, and nearly every chapter read aloud. Today's story. One chat a day, up to 5 messages. Your first sleep night, then one a week. A word for each morning, a journal and saved verses.

A paid plan adds unlimited Bible chat, chat that remembers what you are carrying, a session every night, every past story, longer plans and offline listening. Private for your first 50 messages each day; after that, chat keeps going, just not private. Your private chats are never used to train AI.

Scripture is quoted word for word from the King James Version, and in Spanish from the Reina-Valera 1909. Chat answers are written by AI and marked AI; what you type in free chat goes to free AI providers that may learn from it. There are no ads, and the app never asks for your name.

No trial. A paid plan renews monthly or yearly until you cancel. Google Play shows the price and renewal terms before you buy. Cancel any time in the Play Store app: tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
