Title: Peace Be Still
Short description: Whispered sessions to fall asleep to, a word for morning, and a calm chat.

Full description:
A great calm, every night.

Peace Be Still is one quiet place for the end of the day and the start of the next: whispered sessions to fall asleep to, a word for the morning, and a calm chat to talk things through. A calm bedtime routine.

Choose your way in: God's Word, or mindset & imagination. Each one is its own space, and you can switch any time in Settings.

Free, and it stays free: the whole Bible to read, and nearly every chapter read aloud. Today's story. One chat a day, up to 5 messages. Your first sleep night, then one a week. A word for each morning. Basic 7-day plans, saved verses, a journal, a streak that never scolds, cards to share, and one gentle reminder a day if you want it.

A paid plan adds unlimited Bible chat, chat that remembers what you are carrying, a session every night, sessions made for you, a night you write yourself to fall asleep to (18 and over), every past story, longer plans, declaration topics and offline listening. Unlimited Bible chat. Private for your first 50 messages each day; after that, chat keeps going, just not private. Your private chats are never used to train AI.

Scripture is quoted word for word from the King James Version; in Spanish from the Reina-Valera 1909, in Korean from the 개역한글 (1961) and in Chinese from the 1919 Chinese Union Version. The screens and chat also speak Korean and Chinese; the whispered audio and the stories are in English and Spanish.

Chat answers are written by AI and marked AI; what you type in free chat goes to free AI providers that may learn from it. There are no ads, and the app never asks for your name.

No trial. A paid plan renews monthly or yearly until you cancel. Google Play shows the price and renewal terms before you buy. Cancel any time in the Play Store app: tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
