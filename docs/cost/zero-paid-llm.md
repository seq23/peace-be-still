# Zero paid LLM — peace-be-still

Owner policy (updated 21 Nov 2026): the website and search-loop drafting are $0. The app's paid chat lane, any chat message the safety check flags, and Made for you's fallback (when free models are busy or a free draft misses after one revision) use a billed private model, all under one cap of $100 a month app-wide (`PAID_AI_MONTHLY_CAP_USD`, `docs/OPERATIONS.md`); free chat never uses the billed key. The website makes no model calls. Model use: the content gate's verse read, and the search-loop engine drafting question pages:

| Call site | What it does | Models | Guard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search Loop (`.github/workflows/search-loop.yml`, engine `seq23/search-loop`) | Drafts question pages | the `:free` OpenRouter models in `config/llm_routing.json`, then the engine's other $0 lanes (Gemini no-billing project, Workers AI inside its free daily allowance) when their secrets are set | the engine fails closed on any unknown or non-zero cost (`max_cost_usd: 0`, no paid fallback) |
| Verse read (`scripts/content/verse-read.mjs`, run by hand inside the vault) | Reads each Imagination Night for Bible wording in any translation; the verdict is recorded in `content/src/reviews/verse-read.tsv`, so CI makes no call | `draft.mjs` `GEMINI_TEXT_MODELS` on the app's own free Gemini project (`PBS_GEMINI_API_KEY`) | the route stops `NOT_FREE_TIER` on a billed key; no read = the night is held, never passed |
| Made for you (`worker/src/madeForYou.ts`, `madeForYou.mount.ts`) | Drafts and judges a subscriber's session overnight | free rungs first, strongest first (`config.rungOrder`: free Gemini flash, OpenRouter `:free`, then flash-lite and Workers AI), each under half of chat's daily cap; a free model drafts and revises once, and the next revision or any redraft goes to `config.paid`: DeepSeek v4.1 Flash on OpenRouter zero-retention (US hosts only, data collection denied, never DeepSeek's own API), at most 30 drafts and 10 judgments a day | its cost is metered into chat's `paid_spend_micro`; at `PAID_AI_MONTHLY_CAP_USD` the paid rung is left out and the request waits |

To refresh the model list, fetch `https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models` (no key), keep models whose prompt and completion prices are `"0"`, and update `models` and `pricing_snapshot.date`.
