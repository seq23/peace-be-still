# Operations: the chat Worker

What an operator can do and what the Worker reports. The code is in `worker/src` (controls in `controls.ts`, crisis in `crisis.ts`).

## Pause one account's chat (kill switch)

`POST /api/admin/chat-block` with `Authorization: Bearer $CHAT_ADMIN_TOKEN` and `{"installId": "...", "reason": "short-code", "hours": 72}` (leave out `hours` to pause until lifted). `DELETE` with `{"installId": "..."}` lifts it. That person sees "Chat is paused on this account" and the support link. With no `CHAT_ADMIN_TOKEN` the route answers `CHAT_ADMIN_TOKEN_MISSING` (503).

## Store review access (no accounts, one code)

Google Play's reviewer reaches the paid plan with a 10-digit code (Android only: App Review buys in the sandbox, and Apple forbids an own unlock, Guideline 3.1.1), never a free switch: `worker/src/review.ts`, tested in `worker/test/review.test.ts` and the paid-screen render tests.
- **Turn on for a review:** put both Worker secrets (from the Boss OS vault, through a pipe): `REVIEW_CODE` (exactly 10 digits, vault `PBS_PLAY_REVIEW_CODE`) and `REVIEW_UNTIL` (the date the window closes, e.g. `2026-12-05`). Then the Android price screen shows a collapsed "Have a review code?" link under the plans; it opens the "App review code" box, and the right code makes the Worker ask RevenueCat (`REVENUECAT_SECRET_KEY`) for a promotional grant of the `paid` entitlement to that install until min(7 days, `REVIEW_UNTIL`). Put the code and the steps (Settings > A paid plan > Have a review code? > type the code > Use this code) in Play Console > App content > App access.
- **At most 10 devices per code version** (`REVIEW_CODE_VERSION` in wrangler.jsonc, `REVIEW_MAX_DEVICES` to override, max 50): `review_grant` keeps a keyed hash of each install (deleted 7 days after its grant ends); an install already in redeems again without a new slot. The 11th install gets 403 `USED_UP` ("used on too many devices") and an Open Loops row (`REVIEW_DEVICE_CAP`, needs `BOSS_OPEN_LOOPS_TOKEN`). **Rotate:** new code in the vault and `REVIEW_CODE`, bump `REVIEW_CODE_VERSION` (v2…), update App access.
- **Per IP:** 20 tries a day and 2 new devices a day (`review_ip_day`, keyed daily IP hash, IPv6 by /64, deleted with the day counters); the 3rd new device from one IP gets `USED_UP`.
- **Failure:** RevenueCat 5xx, timeout or a read-only key (403, `REVIEW_RC_KEY_READONLY`) → the Worker revokes promotional grants only (never a purchase), gives the slot back and answers 503; D1 down → 503 before any RevenueCat call.
- **Off:** either secret missing, not 10 digits, no `REVIEW_CODE_VERSION`, or past `REVIEW_UNTIL` → `/api/review` and `/api/review/unlock` answer 404 and call no one, and the box is hidden. `wrangler secret delete REVIEW_CODE` after approval.
- **Guessing:** 5 tries a minute per install (`REVIEW_BURST`), 10 a day per install (`review_try_day`, deleted with the day counters), so one person cannot use up the reviewer's tries; 1,000 a day for everyone as a backstop (`chat_counts.review_tries`); then 429 until tomorrow (Central). Log codes: `REVIEW_UNLOCKED`, `REVIEW_CODE_WRONG`, `REVIEW_INSTALL_DAILY_CAP`, `REVIEW_IP_DAILY_CAP`, `REVIEW_DAILY_CAP`, `REVIEW_IP_SLOT_CAP`, `REVIEW_DEVICE_CAP`, `REVIEW_RC_UNAVAILABLE` and `REVIEW_RC_KEY_READONLY` (503, the app says try again; never unlocked), `REVIEW_DB_UNAVAILABLE`. `scripts/worker-deploy.sh` puts `REVIEW_CODE` from the vault's `PBS_PLAY_REVIEW_CODE` and `BOSS_OPEN_LOOPS_TOKEN` on every deploy.

## Android build and Google Play internal upload

`.github/workflows/android-store-build.yml`, manual only: `eas build --local` (profile `internal`, the upload keystore held by EAS) on a GitHub runner, `scripts/android/check-aab.mjs` (package, versionCode, signature, no blocked permission), `eas submit --path` to the internal track as a draft, then `scripts/android/play-track.mjs` proves it is a draft there and on no other track. The bundle must be signed by the EAS-held upload key (`UPLOAD_SHA1` in the workflow, SHA-1 `94:2D:B9:3B:B4:B7:17:E7:36:2F:D4:34:56:71:69:CF:7D:D2:65:4B`, created 22 Nov 2026 with `eas credentials:configure-build -p android -e internal`). Each secret is set only on the steps that use it, and the Play key file is written just before the upload. The bundle is never an artifact. Repo secrets: `EXPO_TOKEN`, `GOOGLE_PLAY_UPLOAD_SA_JSON`, `REVENUECAT_PBS_ANDROID_PUBLIC_KEY` (`goog_…`; `app.config.ts` refuses a Play build without it: `REVENUECAT_PBS_ANDROID_KEY_MISSING`). Play subscriptions use the App Store ids, each with one base plan (`playBasePlan` in `app/src/config/products.json`); listing text in `store/google-play/listing-en.md` (default, en-US) and `store/google-play/listing-es.md` (es-US translation); `app/test/releaseGate.unit.ts` checks both, and the two App Store listings, against the app's free and paid counts.

## Spend

`GET /api/admin/spend` (same token): the paid-route spend this calendar month (Central), the cap (`PAID_AI_MONTHLY_CAP_USD`, $100) and which alerts fired.

## Named stops and alerts (log codes, no chat text)

| Code | Means | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CHAT_PAID_CAP_80` | Paid AI spend reached 80% of the month's cap | Alert once a month; chat goes on |
| `NAMED STOP CHAT_PAID_MONTHLY_CAP` | Paid AI spend reached the cap | Paid users are offered free chat (not private) behind their yes, as after `PAID_CHAT_MESSAGES` (75) private messages; if free chat has nothing, short private answers (at most `PAID_SHORT_ANSWERS_DAILY`, 50, a person a day; then "Chat is busy right now"). Crisis messages still get the card, and paid users the crisis model |
| `STORY_ART_BUCKET_MISSING` / `STORY_ART_IMAGE_MISSING` | Story artwork (worker/src/storyart.ts, app/src/story): R2 bucket `pbs-story-art` (made by `scripts/worker-deploy.sh`) holds `<storyId>.webp` and `manifest.json` = `{"version": N, "ids": [approved ids]}` | To put an approved image live with no app build: `npx --prefix worker wrangler r2 object put pbs-story-art/<id>.webp --file <file> --content-type image/webp --remote --config worker/wrangler.jsonc`, then the manifest with the id added and `version` raised (a new version is a new image URL; images are cached forever). Remove an id from the manifest to take its image down. Phones re-read the manifest at most daily. No public copy may claim pictures until images are live (site/test/story-claims.test.mjs). |
| `PBS_GIT_CRYPT_KEY_MISSING` / `_INVALID` / `GIT_CRYPT_NOT_INSTALLED` / `GIT_CRYPT_STILL_LOCKED` / `GIT_CRYPT_LOCKED` | The chat prompts, crisis lists and test question sets (the `filter=git-crypt` paths in `.gitattributes`) are still encrypted here | Nothing runs on ciphertext: CI fails at the unlock step, `worker:deploy` and `worker:dev` stop before anything else. Unlock this clone and all its worktrees once: `cd ~/GitHub/boss-os && npm run -s vault:run -- bash ~/GitHub/peace-be-still/scripts/git-crypt-unlock.sh` (key `PBS_GIT_CRYPT_KEY`, base64, in the vault and as the repo secret). Never run `git-crypt unlock` itself (it breaks the other worktrees). `PLAIN_TEXT_COMMITTED` / `COPY` from `scripts/check-encrypted.mjs`: a protected file went in plain, or its text sits in an unencrypted file; history is not rewritten, so text committed before 22 Nov 2026 stays readable in old commits. |
| `DEVICECHECK_KEY_MISSING` | The Worker has no Apple DeviceCheck key (`APPLE_DEVICECHECK_KEY` .p8, `APPLE_DEVICECHECK_KEY_ID`, `APPLE_TEAM_ID`) | Fail closed: no install gets the private first chat; everyone gets the normal free chat behind its consent. The key is in the vault (`APPLE_DEVICECHECK_KEY`, `APPLE_DEVICECHECK_KEY_ID`, `APPLE_TEAM_ID_PBS`); `scripts/worker-deploy.sh`, run under `vault:run`, checks it with Apple (`scripts/devicecheck-probe.mjs`: 400 for a dummy token = good, 401 = NAMED STOP `DEVICECHECK_KEY_REJECTED`, nothing deployed) and puts the three Worker secrets through a pipe on every deploy. `DEVICECHECK_QUERY_FAILED` / `DEVICECHECK_UPDATE_FAILED` / `DEVICECHECK_FAILED`: Apple refused or did not answer; same fail-closed result. |
| `first_private:<yes or reason>` (chat_counts) | The private first chat's decision per install (`POST /api/chat/first`, worker/src/firstprivate.ts): `yes`, `no_devicecheck`, `device_used`, `ip_cap` (3 a network a day), `global_cap` (`FIRST_PRIVATE_PER_DAY`, 300), `cap` (the $100 monthly cap), `devicecheck_error` | The cohort for the 25 Jan paid-conversion decision: `SELECT granted, COUNT(*) AS installs, SUM(paid_at IS NOT NULL) AS paid FROM chat_first_private GROUP BY granted` (rows kept 120 days). `first_private_down` / `first_private_cap`: a first chat stopped and asked for the free-chat yes. |
| `CHAT_SAFETY_CAP_80` / `NAMED STOP CHAT_SAFETY_MONTHLY_CAP` | The safety model's month (`SAFETY_MONTHLY_CAP_USD`, $10) at 80% / used up | Past it the safety model is skipped; intent phrases, the answer model's risk tag and the crisis model's instructions carry on |
| `CHAT_USER_CEILING` | One paid account passed its hidden monthly AI backstop (`CHAT_USER_MONTHLY_USD_YEARLY` $1.33, raise to $1.83 once enrolled in Apple's Small Business Program; `CHAT_USER_MONTHLY_USD_MONTHLY` $4.59; both in worker/wrangler.jsonc, no app update) | Same as after her 75 private messages a day (`PAID_CHAT_MESSAGES`, app/src/gates/index.ts): she is asked once a day whether to keep chatting on free chat (not private); her message is kept and sent on the lane she picks. If the free lane has nothing, the private model answers briefly (`overflow_short`). A paid user never sees a pause; crisis stays on the private crisis model |
| `CHAT_ABUSE_SLOW` / `CHAT_ABUSE_PAUSE` | Floods, repeated messages or jailbreak spam | Slowed, then paused 24 hours |
| `CHAT_PAID_DOWN` | The paid model gave nothing | "Paid chat is down for a moment"; never a free model |
| `CHAT_CRISIS_MODEL_DOWN` | The crisis model gave nothing on an adult's high signal | The card and the fixed check-in still show (a teen never calls the crisis model, so never logs this) |
| `CHAT_NO_FREE_MODEL` | Every free allowance is used up today | "Free chat is resting" |

## Lanes and models (env, `worker/wrangler.jsonc`)

- Free: `providers.mjs` order (Workers AI, Gemini free on `PBS_FREE_GEMINI_API_KEY`, OpenRouter free). Needs the person's consent (`freeConsent`). Teens (13-17, and any token not signed adult) get only the rungs with `trains: false` (`teenProviders`: Workers AI); when it is out or down they get the resting reply, never a training rung (`worker/test/teen-routing.test.ts`).
- Paid: `PAID_PROVIDER` + `PAID_MODEL` on the billed key `PBS_GEMINI_API_KEY` (or Workers AI, or OpenRouter with zero data retention). Never falls back to free.
- Crisis: `CRISIS_MODEL` on the billed key, for any adult conversation the safety check flags; it decays after 10 calm turns or 24 hours.
- Teens and Gemini (her ruling 23 Nov 2026, "Card + hotline only"; Google's terms bar every Gemini route, Vertex included, for services likely used by under-18s): a teen's words never reach a rung marked `google` (`offGoogle` in `worker/src/index.ts`, every Gemini rung and any `google/` model through OpenRouter). Paid is `PAID_PROVIDER` alone, no `PAID_BACKUP_MODEL`; the crisis lane is Workers AI (`teenProviders`); the answer judge has no Google backup. A teen's high signal gets the card, the region's lines and the fixed "Are you safe right now?" words with no model call. Workers AI's answer calls are a small shared daily cap (`WORKERS_AI_ANSWER_CALLS_DAILY`, spent by adults first), so a teen's crisis lane is often out: after the card has shown in that conversation she gets fixed follow-up words (`teenStillHereText`, metric `crisis_lane_down_teen_followup`), never the card and question again unless a new high signal or a not-safe answer comes; her "yes, I'm safe" counts as a calm turn. Each teen gets one model answer a day off the paid lane (`TEEN_AI_ANSWERS_DAILY`, her ruling 23 Nov 2026 "yes, 1 each"), claimed per install in `chat_short_day` under a `teen:` key (cleaned with the day) before the ladder, given back when no model answered; past it the free lane says `teenDailyText` (metric `teen_answer_used`) and the crisis lane the follow-up words above; an unreadable counter is busy (`teen_answer_unread`, log `TEEN_ANSWER_CAP_UNREAD`), never unlimited. Guarded by `worker/test/teen-routing.test.ts`.
