# For when you've lost someone (English, KJV): a stored verse and one short, gentle line in the first person.
# Written by Claude (no free-model draft), 21 Nov 2026. Present and warm; no reason given for a death, no timeline, no "better place".
1 | PSA 34:18 | My heart is broken. I do not have to hide that from God. He comes near to the broken, so I let Him come near to me.
2 | JOH 11:35 | Jesus wept for His friend Lazarus, even knowing what He was about to do. My tears are not a lack of faith. I can cry, and He understands.
3 | MAT 5:4 | I am allowed to mourn. I do not have to rush it, and I do not have to explain it to anyone.
4 | PSA 56:8 | Not one of my tears is lost. God sees every one, and He knows the name of the one I miss.
5 | ECC 3:4 | This is a time to weep. I let it be what it is, without pretending and without hurrying myself.
6 | PSA 31:9 | Some days the grief is in my whole body. I can tell God exactly how heavy it is.
7 | PSA 23:4 | I am walking through a valley I did not choose. I am not walking it alone.
8 | ISA 43:2 | The waters feel deep right now. The Lord does not ask me to cross them by myself.
9 | 2CO 1:3 | I need a comfort I cannot give myself. I ask the God of all comfort for it now, and I let the people who love me help.
10 | REV 21:4 | One day God will wipe away every tear. For now I let mine fall, and I keep loving the one I lost.
