=== core-040-ten-commandments | The Ten Commandments
passage: EXO 19:16-20; EXO 20:1-17
shelf: adult
violence: none
sexual: none
cast: none
N: It was morning on the third day at Mount Sinai. The people were camped at the foot of the mountain. They had left Egypt behind, and now it was time to meet with God. Then the mountain began to change.
S: EXO 19:16
N: Moses led the people out of their camp to stand near the base of the mountain. Everyone looked up at what was happening above them.
S: EXO 19:18
N: The trumpet sound grew louder and louder. Moses spoke, and God answered him with a voice. Then God came down upon the top of Mount Sinai and called Moses up. Moses climbed the mountain, and God began to speak words for the people to hear.
N: Before giving a single rule, God reminded the people of who he was and what he had done. He was the one who had brought them out of slavery in Egypt.
S: EXO 20:2
N: Because he had rescued them and belonged to them as their God, he asked for their whole devotion. They were not to put anything else before him.
S: EXO 20:3
N: God also told them not to carve statues or shape idols. They were not to copy anything in the sky, on the earth, or in the sea. They were not to kneel before those idols or worship them. God told them he cares deeply for his people. He shows mercy to thousands of those who love him and keep his ways.
S: EXO 20:4
N: Next, God spoke about his own name. A name carries weight. God told the people to treat his name with care and respect. They must never use it in an empty or careless way.
S: EXO 20:7
N: Then God gave them a rhythm of work and rest. For six days, they would do all their labor. On the seventh day, everything in the camp would stop. Sons and daughters, servants, visitors, and even work animals would rest. God himself had worked for six days to make the world and everything in it, and then he rested on day seven.
S: EXO 20:8
N: After speaking about how to honor him, God turned to how people treat one another. He began inside the home. Children were to care for and respect their father and mother. Then their days would be long in the land God was giving them.
S: EXO 20:12
N: From there, God set clear boundaries to protect every person in the camp. Human life was precious, and no one was to take another person's life.
S: EXO 20:13
N: God also guarded the bond of marriage. Husband and wife were meant to stay faithful to one another.
S: EXO 20:14
N: He protected what belonged to each household as well. People were not to take anything that belonged to someone else.
S: EXO 20:15
N: Words mattered too, because neighbors needed to trust each other. God told them never to tell lies against a neighbor.
S: EXO 20:16
N: Finally, God looked past outward deeds and spoke to the heart. Before someone steals or lies, envy can grow quietly inside. God told them not to long for anything that belonged to a neighbor. They were not to want a neighbor's house, spouse, servant, or animal.
S: EXO 20:17
N: On that shaking mountain, God gave his people these clear commands to live by. He began by reminding them that he was the one who had brought them out of slavery. From that rescue, he showed them how to love him. He also showed them how to live in peace beside the people around them.
