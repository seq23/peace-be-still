N: Jesus had crossed the Sea of Galilee again in a boat. As soon as he stepped onto the shore, a large crowd gathered around him near the water. People pressed close, eager to see him and hear him.
N: Into that crowd came a man named Jairus. He was a leader of the local synagogue, a man people in town knew and respected. None of his standing mattered to him now. When he saw Jesus, he pushed his way forward and knelt down in the dust before him.
S: MAR 5:22
N: A father's heart was breaking. His young daughter was at home, very sick and close to dying. Jairus begged Jesus for help.
S: MAR 5:23 @jairus after "saying,"
N: Jesus did not hesitate. He turned and started walking toward the man's house.
S: MAR 5:24
N: Every step must have felt slow to Jairus. A thick crowd moved with them down the street, pressing in from every side. When someone you love is sick at home, every minute feels heavy. Then, while Jesus was still speaking on the road, people arrived from Jairus's house with the news every parent dreads.
S: MAR 5:35 @messengers after "which said,"
N: In a moment, hope seemed to go out like a candle in the wind. The messengers thought the journey was over. There was nothing left for a teacher to do now. But Jesus heard what they told the father.
S: MAR 5:36
N: Jesus told the crowd to stay back. He let only Peter, James, and John come with him the rest of the way. The house grew closer. Quietly, Jesus walked on with Jairus and the three disciples.
N: When they reached the house, the quiet broke. A loud crowd was mourning there. The air was full of crying and grief.
S: MAR 5:38
N: Jesus stepped inside into the middle of all that noise. He asked the mourners why they were making such a scene and crying so hard. Then he told them the girl had not died, and that she was only sleeping.
S: MAR 5:39
N: The people were sure she was gone, and they mocked what he said. Jesus sent the noisy crowd outside. When the house was quiet, he brought only the girl's parents and the three disciples into her room.
S: MAR 5:40
N: The room was still now. Jesus went over to her, reached out, and held her hand. Then he spoke to her in her own language.
S: MAR 5:41
N: Right away, life returned to the girl. She stood up and began moving around the room. Everyone there was filled with wonder at what had just happened.
S: MAR 5:42
N: Jesus gave them clear instructions. He told them to keep what had happened quiet. And in the middle of all their shock, he thought of a simple, caring detail. He made sure the girl would be given food.
S: MAR 5:43
N: That is how the story ends, with a quiet room and a meal for a twelve-year-old girl. The news from the house had sounded final. The grief had been loud, and the fear had been real. Yet Jesus asked Jairus not to be afraid, and he proved why. Even death itself listened when Jesus spoke.
