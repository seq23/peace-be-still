=== core-001-peace-be-still | Peace, Be Still
passage: MAR 4:1; MAR 4:35-41
shelf: adult
violence: none
sexual: none
cast: disciples|the disciples|adult|group
N: Church, it had been a long day by the Sea of Galilee. So many people came to hear Jesus that he had to teach them sitting in a boat, just off the shore. And when the evening came, he told his disciples, let us cross over to the other side.
N: They sent the people home and took him with them, just as he was, tired from the day. Other little boats went out with them. And Jesus went to the back of the boat, laid his head down on a pillow, and went to sleep.
N: Now Galilee sits low, with hills all around it, and the wind can come down off those hills quick. That night it did.
S: MAR 4:37
N: These were fishermen, some of them. They knew that water. They knew boats. And they were scared. Not a little worried. Scared for their lives. They bailed and pulled and hollered over the wind, and the boat kept filling up.
N: And where was Jesus? Asleep on a pillow.
S: MAR 4:38 @disciples
N: Hear that question. Master, don't you care? That is not a question from people with no faith. That is a question from people who came to the right one and still could not understand why he was so quiet. Some of us have asked it in a hospital hallway. Some of us have asked it at the kitchen table with the bills spread out. The old saints asked it in the fields, and they kept on singing anyhow.
N: Then he got up.
S: MAR 4:39
N: No long speech to that storm. A few words, and the wind laid down like a fussing baby in its mother's arms. The waves went flat. The whole lake went still, the kind of still you can hear.
S: MAR 4:40
N: He did not ask them to pretend they were never afraid. He asked them where their faith had gone, when he had been right there in the boat with them the whole time.
S: MAR 4:41 @disciples
N: What manner of man is this? That is the question the story leaves with us tonight. My grandmother used to hum "Jesus, Lover of My Soul" when the storms rolled through, that old Wesley hymn about hiding till the storm of life is past. She never told me the storm would not come. She told me who was in the boat. Can I get an amen?
