=== in-01-living-in-the-end | Living in the End
theme: the method
settle: settle1
teaching: Tonight we start with the simplest idea in this practice. It is called living in the end. [pause 3]
teaching: Most of the time, when we want something, we picture the road to it. The worry, the steps, the waiting. Living in the end means skipping ahead in your imagination to the moment after. The moment when the thing you hoped for has already happened, and you are simply living there. [pause 5]
teaching: You do not picture yourself wanting it. You picture yourself having it, in an ordinary way. Not a big celebration. Just a normal evening after it is done. [pause 5]
teaching: The question to ask is a small one. If it were already true, what would I be doing tonight? Where would I be sitting? Who would I call? How would my body feel? [pause 5]
teaching: This is a practice of imagination and feeling. It is not a promise about what will happen, and it does not replace the real steps you take in the daytime. It is a way to spend the last minutes of the day feeling hopeful instead of afraid. [pause 5]
teaching: Choose one wish now. Keep it simple. You do not have to say it out loud. [pause 10]
scene: Picture an evening, some weeks from now. The wish has come true. It is not new anymore. It is just part of your life. [pause 13]
scene: You are at home, at the end of the day. Notice where you are sitting. A couch. A kitchen chair. A porch step. [pause 13]
scene: Notice the light. Maybe it is the last orange light of evening through the window. Maybe it is a lamp. [pause 13]
scene: You are not thinking about the wish. You are just living after it. You are relaxed in a way you have not been in a long time. [pause 15]
scene: Your phone buzzes. It is someone who knows. They are checking in, the way people do when something good has settled into your life. You smile at the screen. [pause 15]
scene: Hear their voice. They say something simple. I am so glad for you. And you say, me too. It still feels good. [pause 18]
scene: Put the phone down. Look around the room. What is different now? Maybe something small. A new key on the counter. A name on a calendar. A letter on the table. [pause 18]
scene: Feel your shoulders. Notice that they are not tight. The worry you used to carry about this is not there. [pause 18]
scene: Take a breath in this scene. Breathe the way someone breathes when a long wait is over. [pause 18]
scene: Stay here. You do not need to add anything. Just stay in the evening after. [pause 23]
scene: If you want, say a quiet sentence in your mind, in the past tense. It happened. It is done. I am glad. [pause 23]
scene: Now let the evening move on a little. You get up and make something warm to drink. Notice the mug in your hands. [pause 18]
scene: You stand at the window. Outside, the street is quiet. A car passes. Someone's porch light comes on. [pause 18]
scene: You think about the time before, when you were still waiting. It feels far away now. Like a season that ended. [pause 21]
scene: You are not proud or anxious. Just settled. This is simply your life now. [pause 21]
scene: Let the scene go a little soft around the edges, the way a room looks when you are almost asleep in it. [pause 23]
close: close1

=== in-02-sats | The Drowsy Moment
theme: the drowsy moment before sleep
settle: settle2
teaching: Tonight is about a very particular moment. The few minutes just before sleep, when your body is heavy and your thoughts start to drift. [pause 5]
teaching: Some people simply call it the drowsy state. It is the moment when you are still awake enough to choose a picture, but relaxed enough that the picture feels real. Arguing and doubting are quieter there. [pause 7]
teaching: The practice is simple. Choose one short scene that would happen after your wish comes true. Something that lasts only a few seconds. A handshake. A hug. A sentence someone says to you. [pause 7]
teaching: Then, as you get drowsy, you repeat that small scene over and over, gently, like a song you hum to yourself. You do not try hard. You let it loop until you drift off inside it. [pause 7]
teaching: Nothing here promises a result. It is a way of ending the day with one hopeful picture instead of a pile of worries. [pause 7]
teaching: Choose your small scene now. Something that takes about as long as one breath. [pause 12]
scene: Let your body be heavier than it was a minute ago. Your arms. Your legs. Your eyelids. [pause 21]
scene: Now bring in your small scene. See where you are standing. See who is with you. [pause 21]
scene: Let it happen once. The handshake. The hug. The sentence. Feel it in your hands, or on your face. [pause 24]
scene: Now let it happen again, the same way. No need to make it bigger. [pause 24]
scene: And again. Let it be a little slower this time. [pause 24]
scene: If you lose it, that is all right. Just start it again from the beginning. [pause 24]
scene: Feel how natural it is. As if it has happened many times already. As if it is a memory. [pause 27]
scene: Let the words in the scene become very simple. Maybe just one word. Congratulations. Thank you. Welcome home. [pause 27]
scene: Say that word in your mind, softly, in the rhythm of your breathing. [pause 29]
scene: Notice how little effort this takes. You are not building anything. You are only remembering a moment. [pause 27]
scene: If another thought comes in, let it walk past, like someone crossing the far end of a room. Then come back to your scene. [pause 27]
scene: The handshake. The hug. The words. Again. [pause 29]
scene: Let the scene get smaller and softer, until it fits inside one breath. [pause 29]
scene: Keep looping it as your thoughts get slow. Let the scene and the sleepiness blur together. [pause 34]
close: close2

=== in-03-revision | Rewriting Today
theme: revising the day
settle: settle1
teaching: Tonight's practice is called revision. It is for the days that did not go the way you wanted. [pause 5]
teaching: Most of us replay a bad moment at night. The argument. The mistake at work. The thing we wish we had said. We play it over and over, and every time we feel worse. [pause 7]
teaching: Revision turns that habit around. You still replay the moment, but this time you change it in your imagination. You let it go the way you wish it had. You hear the kinder words. You see yourself calm. [pause 7]
teaching: This does not erase what happened, and it is not pretending. If you owe someone an apology or a phone call, that is still yours to make tomorrow. Revision is a way to stop carrying the moment to bed with you, and to practice the person you want to be next time. [pause 7]
teaching: Think of one moment from today you would like to rewrite. Something small is best. [pause 12]
scene: Go back to the start of that moment. Notice where you were. The room. The time of day. Who was there. [pause 23]
scene: Let it begin the way it began. But this time, before the hard part comes, take a breath in the scene. [pause 23]
scene: Now let it go differently. If you spoke sharply, hear yourself speaking gently. If you were ignored, see the other person turn and listen. [pause 26]
scene: Hear the new words. Let them be ordinary words. Nothing dramatic. Just the way you wish it had gone. [pause 26]
scene: Watch the other person's face soften. Watch your own shoulders drop. [pause 26]
scene: Let the moment end well. Maybe a nod. A laugh. A thank you. [pause 26]
scene: Now play the new version once more, from the beginning. Let it feel familiar this time. [pause 29]
scene: And once more, slower. Let it become the version you remember. [pause 31]
scene: Notice how you feel toward the other person now. A little softer. A little more patient. [pause 29]
scene: Notice how you feel toward yourself. You were tired today. You did your best. In this version, you let yourself be kind to you as well. [pause 29]
scene: If there is something to make right tomorrow, picture yourself doing it calmly. A short message. A kind word. Then let that go too. [pause 29]
scene: The day is closing like a book. The last page you read was a good one. [pause 31]
scene: When it ends, let it fade. The day is finished, and the last version you saw was a kind one. [pause 31]
close: close1

=== in-04-home | The Key in Your Hand
theme: home
settle: settle2
teaching: Tonight's wish is about home. A place that feels like yours. Maybe that means a new apartment, a house, a quieter street, or simply a room where you feel safe. [pause 5]
teaching: In this practice, we do not picture the search, the paperwork or the moving boxes. We picture a quiet evening after you are settled in. [pause 7]
teaching: The details matter more than the size. The sound of your own door closing. The light switch you have learned to find in the dark. The smell of your kitchen. [pause 7]
teaching: Remember, this is imagination and feeling, not a promise. The real steps still belong to the daytime. Tonight is for the feeling of being home. [pause 7]
teaching: Let a picture of that home come to mind. It does not have to be clear. A feeling is enough to start. [pause 12]
teaching: Some people carry a lot of moving in their lives. Many addresses, many temporary places. If that is you, be gentle with yourself tonight. The point is not the building. It is the feeling of being settled, of not having to pack again. [pause 7]
scene: You are walking up to your door at the end of a long day. Notice what is under your feet. A step. A path. A hallway. [pause 22]
scene: Feel the key in your hand. The weight of it. The little ridges on the metal. You know exactly which one it is without looking. [pause 25]
scene: Turn it in the lock. Hear the click. Push the door open. [pause 22]
scene: Step inside. Notice the first thing you see. A rug. A plant. A window with the evening behind it. [pause 25]
scene: Set your bag down where you always set it down. Kick off your shoes. Nobody is going to tell you to move. [pause 25]
scene: Walk to the kitchen. Run a glass of water and drink it slowly, leaning against the counter. [pause 25]
scene: Hear the quiet of the place. It is your quiet. [pause 28]
scene: Now walk to the bedroom. Turn back the covers of your own bed. Sit on the edge for a moment and look around. [pause 28]
scene: Say it softly in your mind. I live here. I am home. [pause 30]
scene: Before you lie down, walk through the rooms once more. Turn off a lamp. Check the lock. It clicks the same way every night now. [pause 28]
scene: Notice a small thing you chose. A picture on the wall. A blanket on the chair. Your things, in your place. [pause 28]
scene: Hear the sounds of the neighborhood settling down. They are familiar now. They are the sounds of home. [pause 28]
scene: Lie down in that bed, in that room, and let this bed and that one become the same bed. [pause 35]
close: close2

=== in-07-confidence | Walking In Like You Belong
theme: confidence
settle: settle1
teaching: Tonight is about confidence. About walking into a room and feeling that you belong there. [pause 5]
teaching: Confidence is partly a feeling we practice. If we only ever imagine ourselves nervous, small or left out, that is the feeling we rehearse. Tonight we rehearse a different one. [pause 7]
teaching: Choose a place where you usually feel unsure. A meeting. A family gathering. A class. A party. We will walk into it together, in your imagination, as the person you would like to be there. [pause 7]
teaching: This is not about pretending to be someone else. It is about letting yourself feel steady. [pause 7]
teaching: Choose your room now. [pause 12]
teaching: Confidence does not mean never being nervous. Even very confident people feel butterflies. It means you can feel the butterflies and still walk in, still speak, still stay. Tonight we practice that feeling, so it is a little more familiar the next time you need it. Your body learns from what it rehearses, and tonight it can rehearse being steady. [pause 7]
scene: You are standing just outside the door. Notice your feet on the ground. Steady. [pause 22]
scene: Take one slow breath. Feel your back straighten a little, not stiff, just tall. [pause 22]
scene: Now walk in. Notice that nobody gasps or stares. People simply look up, and some of them smile. [pause 25]
scene: Someone says your name like they are glad you came. You say hello back, easily. [pause 25]
scene: Find a seat, or a place to stand. Feel how relaxed your hands are. You are not holding on to anything. [pause 25]
scene: Someone asks your opinion. You answer. Your voice sounds the way you want it to sound. Calm and clear. [pause 25]
scene: Notice the room listening, then the talk moving on. It went fine. Better than fine. [pause 28]
scene: At the end, you walk out into the evening air, and you think, that was easy. I belonged there. [pause 28]
scene: Say it in your mind. I belong. [pause 30]
scene: Now picture the next time you go into that room, a week later. It is even easier. People expect you. [pause 28]
scene: Notice how you stand when you wait in line, how you answer the phone, how you say no when you need to. Steady, in small ways. [pause 28]
scene: You catch your reflection in a window as you pass. You look like someone who is at home in their own life. [pause 28]
scene: Let that steady feeling rest in your body as you drift. [pause 35]

=== in-08-it-is-done | It Is Done
theme: stillness after the wish
settle: settle2
teaching: Tonight is a quieter practice. There is no long scene. Just one feeling, held for a while. [pause 8]
teaching: The feeling is the one you have when something you waited for is finally finished. The test is over. The papers are signed. The call came. You let out a breath you did not know you were holding. [pause 10]
teaching: We call it the feeling of it is done. Not excitement. Relief. Rest. The end of reaching. [pause 10]
teaching: You can bring any wish to this practice. You do not have to picture the details at all. Just the feeling after. [pause 10]
teaching: And, as always, it is a practice for your peace tonight, not a promise about tomorrow. [pause 15]
teaching: Some of us have been reaching for something so long that we have forgotten what it is like to stop. Tonight you can practice stopping. Just for a little while, you can let the wish rest, as if it has already been answered, and let your whole body feel what relief feels like. Then you can sleep with that feeling instead of the reaching. [pause 10]
scene: Think of your wish for a moment. Just name it in your mind. [pause 26]
scene: Now imagine the second after it came true. Not the party. The quiet second after. [pause 29]
scene: Feel your chest loosen. Your stomach unclench. Your hands unfold. [pause 29]
scene: Let out a long breath. The breath of someone who can stop waiting. [pause 29]
scene: In your mind, say very simply, it is done. [pause 34]
scene: Notice there is nothing to check, nothing to chase. You can just lie here. [pause 34]
scene: Say it again, softer. It is done. [pause 34]
scene: Let the words fade, and keep only the feeling. [pause 39]
scene: Picture yourself the next morning, waking up. The first thought is not worry. It is something simple. Oh, that is right. It is done. [pause 34]
scene: Feel yourself stretch, and get up slowly, with no knot in your stomach. [pause 34]
scene: Let that morning feeling come back here, into tonight. [pause 34]
scene: Rest in it. [pause 39]

=== in-09-thank-you-already | Thank You, Already

settle: settle1
teaching: Tonight's practice is about saying thank you. Not for what you have now, though that is good too, but for the wish, as if it has already arrived. [pause 7]

teaching: You might picture writing a thank you note to someone who helped. Or telling a friend how grateful you are. Or simply sitting quietly with a full heart. [pause 9]
teaching: This is imagination and feeling, not a promise. Gratitude tonight is good for its own sake, whatever tomorrow brings. [pause 9]
teaching: Bring your wish to mind, and imagine it is already part of your life. [pause 14]
teaching: Gratitude is a feeling the body knows well. It softens the chest and slows the breath. When we picture our wish with gratitude instead of longing, the picture changes. It stops feeling like something far away and starts feeling like something we are already living with. [pause 9]
scene: You are sitting somewhere comfortable, with a pen and a card in front of you. [pause 24]

=== in-10-congratulations | The Friend Who Congratulates You
