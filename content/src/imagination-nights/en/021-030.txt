=== in-21-the-quiet-house | A Quiet House
theme: peace at home
settle: settle1
teaching: Tonight's wish is a home that feels peaceful. Not a bigger house or a fancier one. Just a place where your shoulders come down when you walk in. [pause 12]
teaching: For a lot of us, home is where the noise is. Bills on the counter. Dishes in the sink. Someone who needs something. Tonight we picture the same rooms, but with the feeling of calm settled into them. [pause 14]
teaching: You can picture the place you live now, or a place you hope to live one day. Either is fine. What matters is the feeling of walking through a door and knowing you are safe to rest. [pause 14]
teaching: If home has not felt safe for you, and someone there frightens you, please reach out to a person trained to help, and let tonight's scene be a place of your own making. [pause 14]
teaching: As always, this is imagination and feeling. It is not a promise about anything outside you. It is a way to let your body learn what a calm evening at home feels like. [pause 8]
teaching: Picture your door now. The color of it. The sound it makes. [pause 15]
scene: It is early evening. You are coming home after a long day. [pause 26]
scene: Put your key in the lock, or push the door open. Step inside. [pause 28]
scene: Notice the first thing you smell. Something clean. Something cooking. The plain smell of your own home. [pause 29]
scene: Set your bag down in its place. Take off your shoes. Feel the floor under your feet. [pause 29]
scene: Walk through the rooms slowly. Everything is where it belongs. Nothing is calling for you. [pause 31]
scene: Turn on one soft lamp. Notice how the light falls on the wall. [pause 31]
scene: Somewhere in the house there is a small sound. A clock. The refrigerator humming. Someone you love moving in another room, at ease. [pause 31]
scene: Sit down in your favorite spot. Let the chair or the couch hold you. [pause 33]
scene: Look around and notice how quiet it is inside you, too. [pause 33]
scene: Say in your mind, this is a peaceful home. I am at rest here. [pause 34]
scene: Let the quiet of that house fold into the quiet of this room. [pause 36]
close: close1

=== in-22-the-bills-on-the-table | The Bills on the Table
theme: calm about money
settle: settle2
teaching: Tonight's scene is about money, and the feeling of being calm about it. [pause 12]
teaching: For many of us, money is the thing we think about at two in the morning. The bill that came in. The payment that is late. The number in the account that never seems to stretch far enough. [pause 14]
teaching: We are not picturing riches tonight. We are picturing something smaller and closer. Sitting at the table on a normal evening, opening the bills, and feeling steady. Each one covered. A little left over, put aside. [pause 14]
teaching: You do not have to know how you get there. That is daytime work, and it is good to get sound advice from someone you trust. Tonight is only for the feeling of the evening when the numbers make sense. [pause 14]
teaching: This is imagination and feeling, not a promise of any result. It is a way to let your body practice a quiet breath in the place where it usually holds tight. [pause 8]
teaching: Bring that table to mind now. The one where the mail always lands. [pause 15]
scene: It is a weeknight. The dishes are done. You sit down at the table with the mail and a cup of something warm. [pause 33]
scene: Notice the pile. A few envelopes. Your phone with the bank app open. [pause 34]
scene: Open the first bill. Read the amount. Feel your chest stay soft. You know it is covered. [pause 36]
scene: Pay it. Watch the screen say paid. Set it aside. [pause 36]
scene: Open the next one. The same steady feeling. Paid. Set aside. [pause 36]
scene: Look at the account when you are done. There is still some there. Not a fortune. Enough. [pause 38]
scene: Move a little into savings. Watch that number grow by a small step. [pause 38]
scene: Lean back in your chair. Notice that your jaw is loose. Your hands are still. [pause 38]
scene: Say in your mind, I am steady. I have enough for tonight. [pause 39]
scene: Stack the paid bills neatly, turn off the kitchen light, and walk to bed with nothing hanging over you. [pause 40]
close: close2

=== in-23-a-friend-who-gets-you | A Friend Who Gets You
theme: friendship
settle: settle1
teaching: Tonight's wish is friendship. The kind where you do not have to explain yourself. [pause 12]
teaching: Grown people do not always talk about being lonely, but many of us are. Friends move away. Life gets busy. Some friendships fade, and making new ones can feel awkward after a certain age. [pause 14]
teaching: Tonight we picture an easy afternoon with a friend who gets you. It might be someone you already know, someone you have lost touch with, or someone you have not met yet. You can leave their face soft if you like. The feeling is what we practice. [pause 14]
teaching: This is not about making any particular person call you. People choose for themselves. It is about letting your body remember what it feels like to be welcomed and understood. [pause 14]
teaching: As always, this is imagination and feeling, not a promise. In the daytime, a small step helps. A text. A class. Saying yes to an invitation. [pause 8]
teaching: Let the idea of that friend come to mind now. [pause 15]
scene: It is a free afternoon. Your phone buzzes. A friend is outside. [pause 26]
scene: You open the door, and they are already smiling. Hear them say your name the way only friends do. [pause 29]
scene: You walk together to a place you both like. A diner. A park bench. A front step. [pause 29]
scene: Sit down. One of you starts a story, and the other laughs before it is even finished. [pause 31]
scene: Notice that you do not have to watch your words. You can be tired. You can be silly. You can be honest. [pause 31]
scene: Tell them something that has been on your heart. Watch them nod. They understand. [pause 33]
scene: They tell you something too. Feel how good it is to be trusted. [pause 33]
scene: The time goes fast. The light changes and neither of you noticed. [pause 33]
scene: At the end, they hug you and say, let us do this again soon. And you know they mean it. [pause 34]
scene: Say in your mind, I am easy to love. I have good people. [pause 34]
scene: Let the warmth of that afternoon stay with you as the room grows dark. [pause 36]

=== in-24-the-talk-that-went-well | The Talk That Went Well
theme: speaking up
settle: settle2
teaching: Tonight is for a conversation you have been putting off. Asking for a raise. Telling a family member how you feel. Setting a matter straight with a friend. [pause 12]
teaching: When we dread a talk, we usually rehearse it going wrong. We play the worst version over and over until our stomach is in knots. Tonight we rehearse it going well. [pause 14]
teaching: The other person still has their own mind, so we do not picture forcing them to agree. We picture you. Calm. Clear. Kind. Saying what you came to say, and walking away glad you said it. [pause 14]
teaching: If the person you need to talk with frightens you, or has hurt you, please do not do this alone. Talk first with someone trained to help, and keep yourself safe. Tonight's scene is for ordinary hard talks, not dangerous ones. [pause 14]
teaching: As always, this is imagination and feeling. It is not a promise about how anyone will respond. It is a way to let your body practice being steady in a moment that usually shakes it. [pause 8]
teaching: Bring the talk to mind now. Just the person and the place. [pause 15]
scene: You are walking toward the place where you will talk. Notice your steps are even. [pause 31]
scene: Take one slow breath before you begin. Feel your feet on the ground. [pause 34]
scene: Sit or stand across from them. See their face. Let it be an ordinary face, not a frightening one. [pause 34]
scene: Hear yourself begin. Your voice is calm. You say the first sentence, and it comes out just right. [pause 36]
scene: Keep going. You say the hard part plainly, without rushing and without apologizing for it. [pause 38]
scene: Notice them listening. Notice that the room is still standing. [pause 38]
scene: Let them answer, however they answer. Feel yourself stay steady. You said what was true. [pause 38]
scene: When it is over, you walk out into the air. Your shoulders are light. [pause 38]
scene: Say in your mind, I spoke up. I can do hard things kindly. [pause 39]
scene: Let the relief of that walk settle into your chest as you rest. [pause 40]
close: close2

=== in-25-a-day-with-nowhere-to-be | A Day with Nowhere to Be
theme: rest
settle: settle1
teaching: Tonight's wish is a simple one, and for many people it feels out of reach. A whole day with nowhere to be. [pause 12]
teaching: Some of us have not had a real day off in years. Even on the days we are home, we are working. Laundry, errands, caring for someone, answering messages. Rest starts to feel like something we have to earn. [pause 14]
teaching: Tonight we picture a day that asks nothing of you. No alarm. No list. Just slow hours, one after another, with time to notice them. [pause 14]
teaching: You do not have to figure out how to make that day happen in real life. That is for another time. Tonight you only have to let yourself enjoy it in imagination, without guilt. [pause 14]
teaching: As always, this is imagination and feeling, not a promise. It is a way to remind your body that rest is allowed, even before the calendar agrees. [pause 8]
teaching: Let yourself picture waking on that day now. [pause 15]
scene: You wake without an alarm. The light is already up. Nobody is calling your name. [pause 26]
scene: Lie still for a while. Notice the covers. The pillow. The plain fact that you do not have to get up. [pause 29]
scene: When you are ready, get up slowly. Make breakfast the long way. Taste it. [pause 29]
scene: Sit by a window with a book, or music, or nothing at all. [pause 31]
scene: Let an hour go by. Notice that you did not check the time. [pause 33]
scene: In the afternoon, take a nap with the window cracked. Hear the street sounds come and go. [pause 33]
scene: Wake up soft and unhurried. Step outside for a minute and feel the air. [pause 33]
scene: In the evening, eat something simple. Wash one plate. Leave the rest for tomorrow. [pause 33]
scene: Notice how your body feels at the end of a day like this. Loose. Full. Quiet. [pause 34]
scene: Say in your mind, I am allowed to rest. [pause 34]
scene: Let that easy day carry you down into this easy night. [pause 36]

=== in-26-what-you-planted | What You Planted
theme: patience
settle: settle2
teaching: Tonight we picture a garden, and the patience it takes to grow one. [pause 12]
teaching: Most good things in life grow slowly. A child. A skill. A savings account. A friendship. A business. Some days it looks like nothing is happening at all, and that is usually when we want to give up. [pause 14]
teaching: Anyone who has grown tomatoes or collards knows the secret. You plant, you water, you wait. Underneath the dirt, things are moving that you cannot see yet. [pause 14]
teaching: Tonight you will picture the day your garden is full, and you get to walk through it. Let the garden stand for whatever you are waiting on in your own life. [pause 14]
teaching: This is imagination and feeling, not a promise of any result. Real gardens have dry spells and real lives do too. But tonight you can rest with the feeling of patience rewarded, and let your body practice waiting without worry. [pause 8]
teaching: Bring to mind something you have planted in your life, and are waiting on. [pause 15]
scene: It is a warm morning. You walk out to the garden with a cup in your hand. [pause 28]
scene: Notice the green. Rows of leaves taller than you remember. [pause 31]
scene: Kneel down. Feel the soil, cool and soft. Smell the earth. [pause 31]
scene: Find the first thing ready to pick. Turn it in your fingers. You grew this. [pause 33]
scene: Remember the days it was only dirt, and you watered it anyway. [pause 35]
scene: Walk the rows. Here is something you almost gave up on. It came up after all. [pause 35]
scene: Fill a basket slowly. There is more than you need. [pause 35]
scene: Picture giving some away. A neighbor at the fence. A friend who stops by. [pause 35]
scene: Sit on the step with the basket at your feet. The sun is on your arms. [pause 36]
scene: Say in your mind, I can wait well. Good things take their time. [pause 36]
scene: Let the garden grow quiet around you, and let the evening come down over it. [pause 38]

=== in-27-a-kind-no | A Kind No
theme: boundaries
settle: settle1
teaching: Tonight's scene is about a small word that a lot of us find hard to say. No. [pause 12]
teaching: Many women especially are raised to say yes. Yes to the extra shift. Yes to the favor. Yes to the person who always asks and never offers. Over time, all those yeses can leave us tired and quietly resentful. [pause 14]
teaching: Saying no is not being unkind. It is telling the truth about what you can give. A kind no protects the yes you want to give with your whole heart. [pause 14]
teaching: Tonight you will picture saying no to something, gently and clearly, and feeling at peace afterward. Not guilty. Not shaking. At peace. [pause 14]
teaching: As always, this is imagination and feeling. It is not a promise about how anyone will react. If someone in your life will not accept a no, and that frightens you, please talk with a person trained to help. [pause 8]
teaching: Think of one request you would like to say no to. Just name it to yourself. [pause 15]
scene: Your phone rings, or someone comes to your door. You already know what they are about to ask. [pause 26]
scene: Notice the old feeling start to rise. The quick yes on the tip of your tongue. [pause 29]
scene: Take one breath first. Feel your feet on the floor. [pause 29]
scene: Hear yourself say it. Thank you for thinking of me. I cannot do that this time. [pause 31]
scene: Notice that your voice is warm and steady. You do not explain for a long time. You do not apologize three times. [pause 33]
scene: Let them answer. Maybe they understand right away. Maybe they are surprised. Either way, you stay calm. [pause 33]
scene: Say goodbye kindly. Put the phone down, or close the door. [pause 33]
scene: Now notice the time you just kept. An evening. A rest. A little room to breathe. [pause 33]
scene: Feel how light your chest is. You told the truth, and you were kind. [pause 34]
scene: Say in your mind, my no is kind. My yes is true. [pause 34]
scene: Let that freed-up evening stretch out in front of you, and drift into it. [pause 36]
close: close1

=== in-30-the-porch-light | The Porch Light

teaching: Over these nights you have pictured many wishes. A home, work you love, people who are good to you, a quiet heart. Tonight we bring them together in one evening, many years from now, when you sit on a porch and look back. [pause 14]

teaching: You will not fill in every detail. Let the evening be soft around the edges. What matters is the feeling of looking at your life and finding it good. [pause 14]
teaching: As always, this is imagination and feeling, not a promise. Life will write its own lines. But tonight you can rest in the picture of a life well lived, and let that be enough for now. [pause 8]
teaching: Picture the porch now. The boards. The railing. The light by the door. [pause 15]
scene: It is a summer evening, many years from now. You are sitting on a porch in a comfortable chair. [pause 27]
scene: Notice the sounds. Crickets. A screen door somewhere. Children laughing down the street. [pause 30]
scene: Someone you love comes out and sits beside you. Neither of you needs to talk. [pause 30]
scene: Look out at the yard. The trees are tall now. You remember when they were small. [pause 32]
scene: Let your mind drift over the years. A home you made. Work you were proud of. Friends who stayed. [pause 34]
scene: Think of the hard seasons too. Notice that you came through them. Notice who you became. [pause 34]

scene: Someone calls goodnight from the sidewalk, and you wave. [pause 34]
scene: Feel how full your heart is. Not because everything was perfect, but because it was yours. [pause 35]
