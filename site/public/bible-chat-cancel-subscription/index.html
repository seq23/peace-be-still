How to cancel a Bible chat subscription, and ask for a refund

To cancel a Bible chat subscription, cancel it with the store that bills you: in iPhone Settings if you paid through the App Store, or in the Google Play app if you paid on Android. Deleting the app does not cancel it. To ask for money back, Apple uses reportaproblem.apple.com and Google Play has its own refund request.

Quick answer

iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › the app › Cancel Subscription. Android: Google Play › profile icon › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › the app › Cancel subscription. Refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com (Apple) or Google Play's refund request.

What this page is best for: anyone who wants to stop a Bible chat app from charging again, on any app.

How do I cancel a Bible chat subscription on iPhone?

Apple's steps (Apple Support, “Cancel a subscription from Apple”):

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Tap the Bible chat app's subscription.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription. If there is no Cancel button, it is already cancelled.

How do I cancel on Android?

Google's steps (Google Play Help, “Cancel, pause, or change a subscription”):

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose the Bible chat app's subscription.
  4. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the steps.

Can I get a refund?

On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose “Request a refund”, pick the reason and the purchase. Apple decides each request. On Android, Google Play's refund policy explains when you can ask Google for a refund and when you need to ask the app's developer. If you paid on the app's own website, ask the developer.

What if I am still in a free trial?

Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, as Apple advises, so it does not turn into a paid plan. You can usually keep using it until the trial's end date.

What if I was charged after I cancelled?

Some App Store reviewers in 2026 report a charge after they believed they had cancelled, across several Bible and prayer apps. The usual reasons are cancelling in the wrong place (deleting the app, or cancelling on a website when Apple bills you), a second Apple ID or Google account, or a trial cancelled under 24 hours before it ended. Check each place you could be billed, keep a screenshot of the red expiry date, then use the refund routes above. Frequently asked questions

Does deleting a Bible chat app cancel the subscription?

No. The App Store or Google Play bills it, so it keeps renewing until you cancel in iPhone Settings or Google Play.

Why can't I find the subscription?

Check you are signed in with the Apple ID or Google account you used. If you paid on a website, cancel it there. On iPhone, no Cancel button means it is already cancelled.

Will I lose access straight away?

Usually not. After you cancel, you can normally use the subscription until the end of the period you already paid for.
