How do I know if a Christian AI is trustworthy?

  1. Exact scripture. Verses come from a stored Bible, with book, chapter and verse; the AI never writes them.
  2. Labelled as AI. Every answer says it is AI. It is not a pastor or a person.
  3. Never speaks as God or Jesus. It can point to what Jesus said; it never says “I am” in his name.
  4. Crisis help first. Words about self-harm or abuse get the 988 Lifeline and real help, not an AI reply.
  5. Honest about data. It tells you where your words go before you type.
  6. No outcome promises. No money, healing or results for prayers or payments.

Frequently asked questions

Is it okay for Christians to use AI?

Many Christians use AI as a tool, like a concordance or a search engine. Test what it says against scripture (Acts 17:11), keep praying and reading the Bible itself, and stay in your church.

Can a Christian AI replace a pastor?

No. An AI can point you to scripture and help you put words to a prayer. It cannot know you, visit you, or carry pastoral responsibility. A good one says so.
