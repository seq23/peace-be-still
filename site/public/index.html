How does Peace Be Still work?

The app asks one question, “What do you need tonight?”, and you pick one of two doors. Each door keeps to itself, and you can change your pick later in Settings.

With God's Word

Scripture, prayer and Bible stories, read and spoken. Every verse is the King James Version, word for word, with book, chapter and verse.

  • The whole King James Bible to read, and a growing set of chapters read aloud
  • A Bible chat that answers with real verses
  • A short Bible story each day
  • Whispered Psalms at bedtime

With mindset & imagination

Nightly imagination and declarations. You write a scene you hope for, then rest inside it as a whispered night session, and say a short declaration out loud, morning and night.

  • Whispered night sessions built from your own scene
  • One line to say out loud each morning
  • King James verses woven in, only if you turn them on

What is a Psalm Night?

A Psalm Night is a whispered reading of a Psalm for bedtime. The screen is always dark, it dims after ten seconds, and a sleep timer fades the voice out. Nothing plays next and nothing is offered.

The verse on screen is the King James text, word for word.

Frequently asked questions

What is Peace Be Still?

Peace Be Still is a phone app for the end of the day. It opens with one question, “What do you need tonight?”, and two doors: God's Word, with the King James Bible, whispered Psalms for sleep, Bible chat and Bible stories; and Mindset, with nightly imagination sessions and declarations. It is coming soon.

Is Peace Be Still free?

Yes. The free lane stays free: the whole King James Bible to read and hear, today's story, one chat a day of up to 10 messages, your first sleep night and then one a week, and a daily declaration. The paid lane is $7.99 a month or $39.99 a year and adds unlimited chat, a session every night and every past story.

When does Peace Be Still launch?

Peace Be Still is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. There is no launch date yet. When the app is live, this page will link straight to it.

Which Bible does Peace Be Still use?

The King James Version, quoted word for word from a stored copy, with book, chapter and verse. The AI never writes a verse from memory. The Spanish journey, Mi Refugio, uses the Reina-Valera 1909.

Is there a free trial?

No. There is no trial that turns into a charge and no weekly plan. The free lane is free for good. The paid lane starts only when you choose it, with the price in dollars shown before you tap, and you can cancel in two taps.

Is the chat a real pastor?

No. The chat is AI, and every answer is labelled as AI. It is not a pastor or a person. Verses in its answers come from the stored King James text, and before your first chat the app tells you where your words go.
