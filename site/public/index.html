Frequently asked questions

What is Peace Be Still?

Peace Be Still is a phone app for the end of the day. It opens with one question, “What do you need tonight?”, and two doors: God's Word, with the King James Bible, whispered Psalms for sleep, Bible chat and Bible stories; and Mindset, with daily declarations, Imagination Nights and a calm chat. It is now on the App Store.

Is Peace Be Still free?

Yes. The free version stays free: one chat a day of up to 5 messages, your first sleep night and then one a week, a daily declaration and a journal; in God's Word, also the whole King James Bible and today's story. A paid plan is $7.99 a month or $39.99 a year.

Is the chat private?

With a paid plan, yes: private for the first 50 messages each day and never used to train AI; after that, chat keeps going, just not private. Free chat uses a free AI service that may keep what you type, and the app always asks first.

Is Peace Be Still out yet?

Yes. It is on the App Store for iPhone: tap the App Store button on this page, or search “Peace Be Still” in the App Store. It is coming soon to Google Play. There is no waitlist.

Which Bible does the God's Word door use?

The King James Version, quoted word for word from a stored copy, with book, chapter and verse. The AI never writes a verse from memory. The Spanish journey, Mi Refugio, uses the Reina-Valera 1909.

Is the Mindset door religious?

No. Mindset has no religious content: daily declarations, imagination at night and a calm chat. Each door keeps to itself.

Is there a trial?

No. There is no trial that turns into a charge. The free version is free for good. A paid plan starts only when you choose it, with the price in dollars shown before you tap, and you can cancel in two taps.

Is the chat a real person?

No. The chat is AI, and every answer is labelled as AI. It is not a pastor, a counselor or a person.
