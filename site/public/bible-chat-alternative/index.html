Looking for a Bible chat alternative? What to compare first

A Bible chat alternative is another app you can switch to when the one you use costs too much, starts with a trial you did not want, or gets scripture wrong. Before switching, compare five things: the price in dollars, whether there is a trial, whether there is a weekly plan, whether there are ads, and whether the verses come from a real stored Bible.

Quick answer

Check the In-App Purchases list on the App Store page (it shows every price), read the description for the words “free trial” and “weekly”, and ask the chat for a verse you know by heart to see if it quotes it exactly.

What this page is best for: people comparing Bible chat apps on price, trial and accuracy before they pay.

What should I look for in a Bible chat app?

  1. The price in dollars. On iPhone, scroll to “In-App Purchases” on the App Store page. It lists every price the app can charge.
  2. A trial. A free trial usually becomes a paid plan unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.
  3. A weekly plan. Weekly plans add up: $4.99 a week is about $259 a year.
  4. Ads. Check the description and the app's privacy label.
  5. Scripture accuracy. Ask for a verse you know. A good app quotes it word for word from a named translation.
