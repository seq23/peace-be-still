Privacy policy

Effective: November 20, 2026

This policy covers the Peace Be Still app and the website peacebestillapp.com. Peace Be Still is made by Spry Labs, the trade name of Redwood Reserve Holdings LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company. Write to info@peacebestillapp.com with any privacy question or request.

The short version: there is no account, we never ask for your name, there are no ads, and we do not sell or share personal information. What you type into chat goes to free AI providers, which may keep it and learn from it.

What stays on your phone

Your birth year, the door you chose, your settings, saved verses, highlights, journal entries, your streak and anything you record in your own voice stay on your phone. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Chat

Chat is optional. Before your first chat, the app asks for your permission and tells you where your words go.

Each chat message travels through our service to an AI provider, which writes the answer. The providers, tried in this order, are Cloudflare Workers AI, Google Gemini (free tier) and free models reached through OpenRouter. These providers may retain and use what you type to improve their services, and people at those companies may read it. Please leave out names and anything that identifies you or someone else.

Each chat message is sent with your age group (teen or adult), the door you chose, the chat voice and the language, so the answer fits; none of these is linked to your name, because the app never asks for it. Your birth year itself never leaves your phone.

Every message first goes through a word-based safety check in our own code. When that check recognizes a message as being about self-harm or abuse, our code answers with crisis lines and the message is not sent to any AI model. Every other message is first read by a safety model on Cloudflare Workers AI before an answer is written.

We do not store chat text. It passes through our service to the provider and is gone from our service once the answer is returned.

Paid chat memory

If you are in the paid lane, chat can remember what you are carrying. That memory is a short summary, at most 1,000 characters, stored on our service under an anonymous install id and sent with your messages so the answers remember. It is kept until you delete it in Settings, or until 30 days after your paid plan ends, whichever comes first. Words about self-harm or abuse are never saved to memory.

Sessions made for you

If you are in the paid lane and ask for a session made for you, the scene or worry you write is sent to our service. We place your words into a session script and make the audio on our own computer with an open-source voice engine; your words for these sessions are not sent to an AI company. Your written words are deleted once the audio is made, and at most 7 days after you send them. The finished audio is kept for you to play until you delete it, or until 30 days after your paid plan ends.

The anonymous install id and limits

To keep chat fair and free, our service creates a random install id the first time you chat. It is not tied to your name, email, phone number or Apple account. Our service uses the install id and a one-way hash of your IP address to count messages each day (1 conversation of up to 10 messages a day in the free lane, up to 100 messages a day in the paid lane) and to stop abuse. So that answers do not repeat, our service also keeps short records of recent answer patterns for each install id: keyed hashes of 8 characters, with no words in them. Those counters and records are deleted within 48 hours. We also keep a count of all chat messages per day, with no text and no identifier, to stay inside the free AI allowances.

Audio and the Bible

The Bible text, the Bible audio and the sleep sessions are files. When your phone fetches one, our host (Cloudflare) sees your IP address in order to deliver it, as any web host does. We do not keep a record of what you listen to or read.

Purchases

Apple handles every purchase. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment card. RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions for us, receives an anonymous app user id, your purchase receipts from Apple, and basic details such as the app version, iOS version and store country, so the app knows whether you are in the paid lane. RevenueCat’s diagnostics and its collection of device identifiers are turned off in the app.

What we never do

No ads, no ad SDKs and no analytics SDKs. The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not sell or share personal information. Browser Do Not Track signals do not apply because there is no tracking to stop.

Children

Peace Be Still asks for a birth year before anything else.

Under 13, the app sends no chat, memory, made-for-you session or install id to our service, and the birth year stays on the device. Audio files are fetched like any web file: the IP address is used only to deliver the file. Purchases go through Apple, with a parent through Family Sharing.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child sent us information, write to info@peacebestillapp.com and we will delete it.

For ages 13 to 17, chat uses the teen voice, and paid upgrades go through Apple’s Ask to Buy when a parent has turned it on.

Where the app is offered

The app is offered in the United States and other countries, but not in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, because one of our free AI providers does not allow its free tier to serve people there.

Keeping it safe

Everything between the app and our service travels over HTTPS. The install id is carried in a signed token so no one else can use your limits or your memory, and IP addresses are stored only as one-way hashes.

The website

The website peacebestillapp.com sets no cookies and has no accounts, ads or tracking scripts. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first visit counter with no cookies, which tells us page views, the page you came from, and your country and device type, in totals. Cloudflare, our host, processes basic details of each visit, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the page and protect the site.

Until November 18, 2026 the site had a form to hear when the app launches. It is closed. For people who signed up before then, we keep the email address, page language, door chosen if any, the page, the date and time, and the sentence agreed to, only to send one launch email. We delete the whole list after that email is sent. To be removed sooner, use the “Remove my email” link you saw after signing up, or the link in the launch email.

Your choices and rights

You can delete paid chat memory and made-for-you sessions in Settings at any time. Apple manages subscriptions in your iPhone’s Settings. For anything else, write to info@peacebestillapp.com: you can ask what we hold about your install id and ask us to delete it, and we will answer by hand within 30 days. We treat you the same whether or not you make a request. These rights apply wherever you live, including California and other states with privacy laws.

Changes

When this policy changes, we post the new date here and show a notice in the app for material changes.
