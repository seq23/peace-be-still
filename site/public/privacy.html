Privacy

This website keeps one thing about you, and only if you give it to us: the email you leave to hear when the app launches. Last updated 18 Nov 2026.

The launch list

If you fill in “Tell me when it launches”, we keep:

  • your email address;
  • the language of the page (English or Spanish);
  • which door interests you, if you chose one;
  • the page you signed up on, the date and time, and the sentence you agreed to.

Why: to send you one email when Peace Be Still is in the App Store and Google Play. We use it for nothing else: no newsletter, no other products, never sold, never shared, and never added to any other list. We delete the whole list after the launch email is sent.

Where: in a database on Cloudflare (our host), in the United States.

How to be removed: use the “Remove my email” link on the page you see after signing up, or the link in the launch email. Removal deletes your row right away.

Everything else

  • No cookies, no accounts and no advertising or tracking scripts.
  • One privacy-first visit counter: Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies, does not follow you across sites, and tells us only page views, the page you came from, and your country and device type, in totals.
  • To stop abuse of the sign-up form, your IP address is checked against a short per-minute limit. We do not store it.

What our host sees

The website is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes basic technical details of each visit, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the page and protect the site from attacks. Cloudflare’s own policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

The app

The Peace Be Still app is not available yet. Its full privacy policy, covering what the app keeps and why, will be published here before the app launches.
