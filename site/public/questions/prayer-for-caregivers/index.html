Prayer for caregivers: short prayers and verses for tired hands

A prayer for caregivers asks God for strength, patience and rest for someone looking after a person who cannot look after themselves: an ageing parent, a spouse with an illness, a child with special needs. It can be prayed by the caregiver in a spare minute, or by friends and church family who want to hold them up.

Quick answer

A one-line prayer for a hard moment: “Lord, give me strength for this hour, gentleness for this person, and rest when the house is quiet.” A verse to keep near: Galatians 6:9, And let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not.

What this page is best for: caregivers of a parent, spouse or child, and anyone who wants to pray for a caregiver.

A short prayer for a caregiver who is tired

Lord, my hands are tired and my heart is stretched thin. Give me strength for the next hour, not the whole week. Make me gentle with the one I care for, and gentle with myself when I fall short. Send help I did not ask for, and let me sleep when the house is quiet. In Jesus' name, amen.

A prayer for a caregiver you love

Father, thank you for the way [name] gives of themselves every day. Carry the weight they carry. Give them people who show up, rest that refreshes, and a moment today when they know they are seen. Protect their health, and let them feel your nearness at the end of the day. In Jesus' name, amen.

What does the Bible say about caring for others?

Scripture treats carrying someone else's load as part of following Christ, and it promises that God carries his people too, all the way to old age.

Bear ye one another’s burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ.

Galatians 6:2 · KJV

And even to your old age I am he; and even to hoar hairs will I carry you: I have made, and I will bear; even I will carry, and will deliver you.

Isaiah 46:4 · KJV

How can a caregiver pray when there is no time?

  • One line, many times. “Lord, help me” at the sink is a prayer.
  • Pray the task. While you give a medicine or fold a blanket, name the person and ask God to hold them.
  • Keep one verse in view. Tape Galatians 6:9 to the fridge or the bathroom mirror.
  • Let others pray. Tell your church or one friend exactly what to pray this week.
  • Listen at night. A psalm read aloud once the house is asleep can be the prayer when you have no words.

When caregiving is too heavy

Caregivers carry a lot, often alone. If you are running on empty, tell your doctor, and ask about respite care through your local agency on ageing or the care team. If you ever feel you cannot go on, in the US call or text 988, any hour; outside the US, findahelpline.com lists a free line near you.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good prayer for a caregiver?

Keep it short and honest: “Lord, give me strength for this hour, gentleness for this person, and rest when the house is quiet.” Short prayers fit a caregiver's day and can be prayed many times.

What Bible verse is for caregivers?

Galatians 6:9 (let us not be weary in well doing) and Isaiah 40:29-31 (He giveth power to the faint) are the most used. Matthew 11:28 and 1 Peter 5:7 are for the days when you are the one who needs care.

How do I pray for someone caring for a sick parent?

Pray for their strength, their sleep, help from others, and their own health. Then tell them you prayed, and offer one practical thing, such as a meal or an hour's sitting.
