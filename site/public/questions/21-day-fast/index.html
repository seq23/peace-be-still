The 21-day fast: prayers and a day-by-day plan

A 21-day fast is three weeks set apart for prayer, in which you give up some or all food, or something else such as social media, and give that time to God. Many churches begin one in the first week of January. It follows Daniel, who was mourning three full weeks and ate no pleasant bread.

Quick answer

Choose your fast, pick a start date (many churches begin on Sunday 3 or Monday 4 January 2027), and pray through one focus and one verse each day using the plan below. Keep it quiet, keep it kind, and end on day 21 with thanksgiving.

What this page is best for: anyone joining their church's January fast, or keeping one alone, who wants a plan for each day's prayer.

Before you start

This page is about prayer. It is not medical advice. Before you give up food, talk with your doctor if you are pregnant or nursing, have diabetes or another health condition, take regular medicine, are under 18, or have ever struggled with eating. You can fast from something other than food instead.

What kinds of fast are there?

FastWhat you give upWhere it comes from
The Daniel fastMeat, sweets and rich food, and often wine; many eat vegetables, fruit, grains and waterDaniel 1:12 and 10:3
A partial fastFood until evening, or one meal a dayJudges 20:26 (fasted that day until even)
A full fastAll food, for a short time, with waterEsther 4:16; only with your doctor's okay
A fast from something elseSocial media, TV, games, shopping or sweetsA modern practice, kept in the same spirit

Churches differ on the details of the Daniel fast; follow your church's guide if it has one.

Prove thy servants, I beseech thee, ten days; and let them give us pulse to eat, and water to drink.

Daniel 1:12 · KJV

Week 1 (days 1 to 7): turn back to God

  1. Day 1. Turn back to God with your whole heart. Name why you are fasting, in one sentence.

    Therefore also now, saith the LORD, turn ye even to me with all your heart, and with fasting, and with weeping, and with mourning:

    Joel 2:12 · KJV

  2. Day 2. Ask God for a clean heart. Confess what you already know is wrong.

    Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me.

    Psalm 51:10 · KJV

  3. Day 3. Receive forgiveness. Stop paying for what God has already forgiven.

    If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

    1 John 1:9 · KJV

  4. Day 4. Ask God to search you: habits, words, the way you treat people.

    Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts: And see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting.

    Psalm 139:23-24 · KJV

  5. Day 5. Fast quietly. Keep it between you and God, and keep your face washed.

    But thou, when thou fastest, anoint thine head, and wash thy face; That thou appear not unto men to fast, but unto thy Father which is in secret: and thy Father, which seeth in secret, shall reward thee openly.

    Matthew 6:17-18 · KJV

  6. Day 6. Pray for someone carrying a heavy load, and do one thing to lighten it.

    Is not this the fast that I have chosen? to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, and to let the oppressed go free, and that ye break every yoke?

    Isaiah 58:6 · KJV

  7. Day 7. Rest on God's mercy. End the first week with thanks, not a score.

    It is of the LORD’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.

    Lamentations 3:22-23 · KJV

Week 2 (days 8 to 14): listen

  1. Day 8. Read how Daniel kept his three weeks. Ask God for understanding.

    In those days I Daniel was mourning three full weeks. I ate no pleasant bread, neither came flesh nor wine in my mouth, neither did I anoint myself at all, till three whole weeks were fulfilled.

    Daniel 10:2-3 · KJV

  2. Day 9. When you are hungry, read instead. Let the hunger send you to the Word.

    But he answered and said, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God.

    Matthew 4:4 · KJV

  3. Day 10. Ask God to show you his way for one decision in front of you.

    Shew me thy ways, O LORD; teach me thy paths. Lead me in thy truth, and teach me: for thou art the God of my salvation; on thee do I wait all the day.

    Psalm 25:4-5 · KJV

  4. Day 11. Hand over your own plan. Trust him with the part you cannot see.

    Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.

    Proverbs 3:5-6 · KJV

  5. Day 12. Ask plainly for wisdom, for work, money, family or church.

    If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.

    James 1:5 · KJV

  6. Day 13. Wait on the Lord. Pray for strength for the second half.

    But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.

    Isaiah 40:31 · KJV

  7. Day 14. Be still. Ten minutes of silence before you pray a word.

    Be still, and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the heathen, I will be exalted in the earth.

    Psalm 46:10 · KJV

Week 3 (days 15 to 21): pray for others and go forward

  1. Day 15. Pray like Esther, for your people. Name your family and your church.

    Go, gather together all the Jews that are present in Shushan, and fast ye for me, and neither eat nor drink three days, night or day: I also and my maidens will fast likewise; and so will I go in unto the king, which is not according to the law: and if I perish, I perish.

    Esther 4:16 · KJV

  2. Day 16. Pray for the right way for your household and your children this year.

    Then I proclaimed a fast there, at the river of Ahava, that we might afflict ourselves before our God, to seek of him a right way for us, and for our little ones, and for all our substance.

    Ezra 8:21 · KJV

  3. Day 17. Pray for leaders: your pastor, your city, your country.

    I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men; For kings, and for all that are in authority; that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty.

    1 Timothy 2:1-2 · KJV

  4. Day 18. Pray for a neighborhood or city that needs to turn to God.

    So the people of Nineveh believed God, and proclaimed a fast, and put on sackcloth, from the greatest of them even to the least of them.

    Jonah 3:5 · KJV

  5. Day 19. Give God every worry, one by one, with thanks.

    Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

    Philippians 4:6-7 · KJV

  6. Day 20. Leave last year behind. Press on to what is ahead.

    Brethren, I count not myself to have apprehended: but this one thing I do, forgetting those things which are behind, and reaching forth unto those things which are before, I press toward the mark for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus.

    Philippians 3:13-14 · KJV

  7. Day 21. Give thanks for the 21 days, and for the new thing God is doing.

    Remember ye not the former things, neither consider the things of old. Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth; shall ye not know it? I will even make a way in the wilderness, and rivers in the desert.

    Isaiah 43:18-19 · KJV

How do you end a 21-day fast?

With thanks. On day 21, write down what you prayed for and what you noticed God doing, even small things. Many churches close with a service of praise or communion. Go back to your usual meals gently, the way your doctor advises, and keep one habit from the fast, such as reading before breakfast.

For his anger endureth but a moment; in his favour is life: weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning.

Psalm 30:5 · KJV

Frequently asked questions

When does the January 2027 fast start?

Most churches choose the first Sunday or Monday of the year. In 2027 that is Sunday 3 January or Monday 4 January, so a 21-day fast ends on Saturday 23 or Sunday 24 January. Follow your church's dates if it announces them.

What can you eat on a Daniel fast?

Churches differ, but most follow Daniel 1:12 and 10:3: vegetables, fruit, whole grains, beans and water, with no meat, sweets or rich food. Many also leave out coffee and wine. Check your church's guide, and your doctor if you have a health condition.

Is fasting commanded in the Bible?

Jesus speaks of fasting as something his followers will do: when ye fast (Matthew 6:16), not “if”. The Bible gives no single rule for how long or what to give up, so churches and believers choose with prayer.

What if I break my fast?

Start again at the next meal or the next day. A fast is not a test you pass or fail; it is time given to God. Lamentations 3:22-23 says his mercies are new every morning, and that includes day 9 of a fast.
