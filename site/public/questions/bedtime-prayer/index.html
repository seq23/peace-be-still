A simple bedtime prayer for children, grown-ups and families

A bedtime prayer is the short prayer said at the end of the day, lying down or kneeling by the bed. A simple one for a child: “Thank you, God, for today. Keep me safe tonight. Bless my family. Amen.” For a grown-up, add one thing you are handing over. The habit matters more than the wording.

Quick answer

Children: three lines they can say alone (thank you, keep me, bless). Grown-ups: thank, hand over, rest. Families: one line each, then the same verse together every night, such as Psalm 4:8.

What this page is best for: parents setting up a bedtime routine, and adults who want to start praying before sleep again.

What is the bedtime prayer most people learned as children?

Many American families taught “Now I lay me down to sleep”. Its newer, gentler form goes:

Now I lay me down to sleep, I pray the Lord my soul to keep. Watch over me throughout the night, and keep me safe till morning light. Amen.

Traditional children's prayer

It is not scripture, so it is not quoted from the King James Bible. It is a family prayer, and you can change the words to suit your child.

A bedtime prayer for a child

Short lines a four-year-old can repeat after you, and a ten-year-old can say alone:

  1. Thank you, God, for today.
  2. Thank you for (one thing from the day: a friend, a meal, a game).
  3. Please keep me safe tonight.
  4. Bless Mommy, Daddy, Grandma, and (anyone they name).
  5. Amen.

Let your child fill in line 2 and line 4. That is where the prayer becomes theirs.

A bedtime prayer for grown-ups

“Lord, thank you for carrying me through today. I give you what I did not finish and what I could not fix. Watch over the people I love. Let me rest in you tonight. Amen.”

How do we pray together as a family at night?

  • Keep it under five minutes so nobody dreads it.
  • Each person says one thank-you and one person they are praying for.
  • End with the same verse every night. Children learn it by heart within a couple of weeks.
  • Skip the lecture. Bedtime prayer is not the moment to correct the day.

Is it okay to fall asleep listening to the Bible?

Yes. Plenty of families play a short passage or a psalm after prayer and let it run while children drift off. Use a sleep timer so the audio stops on its own, and turn off autoplay so a new video does not start.

What else do people ask?

What is a good bedtime prayer for kids?

A short one they can say alone: “Thank you, God, for today. Please keep me safe tonight. Bless my family. Amen.” Let them add one thing they are thankful for and the names of people they love.

What is the blessing in Numbers 6?

It is the priestly blessing that Aaron spoke over Israel: The LORD bless thee, and keep thee: The LORD make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee: The LORD lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace. Many parents pray it over a child at bedtime.

Should my child kneel to pray at night?

Kneeling is a good habit if your family likes it, but God hears a child lying under the covers just the same. Pick whatever keeps the prayer happening.

Is “Now I lay me down to sleep” in the Bible?

No. It is a traditional children's prayer, not a Bible verse. Many families now use a gentler last line, “keep me safe till morning light”.
