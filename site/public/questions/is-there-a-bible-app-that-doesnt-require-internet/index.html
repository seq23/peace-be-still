Is there a Bible app that does not require internet?

Peace Be Still is a coming-soon phone app designed for your nightly practice. It allows you to read and hear the whole King James Bible without needing a constant internet connection. You can keep your focus on the Word of God in the quiet of your home, morning or night, without interruptions.

Published 18 November 2026 · Peace Be Still

Reading the Word without a connection

Many people look for a way to read scripture when they are away from home or when the internet signal is weak. A reliable Bible app should provide access to the text regardless of your connection status. Peace Be Still is built to support your daily habit of reading or listening to the King James Bible. You can spend time in the Psalms or the Gospels whenever you feel the need for quiet reflection. This approach ensures that your time with the Lord remains steady and undisturbed by technical issues. Whether you are in a remote area or simply want to avoid data usage, having the text stored on your device is a helpful feature for any believer.

The app includes the whole King James Bible to read, and a growing set of chapters read aloud. You might choose to listen to whispered Psalms for sleep with a sleep timer, known as Psalm Nights. These recordings are meant to accompany your nightly practice. You will find that having these resources available offline makes it easier to keep your routine consistent. There is no need to worry about a sudden loss of signal while you are reading. The app is designed to be a companion for your quiet moments.

  • Access the whole King James Bible offline.
  • Listen to whispered Psalms for sleep.
  • Use the sleep timer for your nightly practice.
  • Read Bible stories told in a Black church voice in Hush Harbor.
  • Access Mi Refugio in Reina-Valera 1909.

Understanding our pricing and access

We believe in transparency regarding how our app is built and paid for. Peace Be Still offers a free version forever for those who wish to use the basic features. If you choose to support the work, there is a paid plan for $7.99 a month or $39.99 a year. We do not offer a trial period, and there are no surprise charges or hidden fees. We also do not include any advertisements in the app. This allows you to focus entirely on the scripture without distraction. You will not find any promotional content interrupting your prayer or reading time.

Our goal is to provide a simple, unhurried space for your daily walk with God. By keeping the app free of ads and trials, we ensure that your experience remains focused on the text. You can trust that the app will function as described. We are working to bring this to the App Store and Google Play soon. Please look for Peace Be Still in the App Store and Google Play.

A space for the whole family

We want to serve families who prefer to read in different languages. Mi Refugio provides a space for Spanish-speaking families to engage with the Reina-Valera 1909. This is part of our commitment to making the Word accessible to everyone. You can use the Bible chat, which is AI and clearly labelled as such, to ask questions. The chat quotes KJV verses word for word. This feature is intended to help you find specific passages quickly. It is a tool for your study, not a replacement for your own reading of the Holy Scriptures.

In addition to the text, we provide Bible stories told in a Black church voice in Hush Harbor. These stories are meant to help you connect with the history and the people of the Bible. We hope these resources help you in your daily practice. Whether you are reading alone or with your children, these tools are here to support your faith. We invite you to explore these features once the app is available.

Scripture for your quiet moments

The Bible is full of promises and guidance for every season of life. Psalm 46:10 says, "Be still, and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the heathen, I will be exalted in the earth." This verse reminds us of the importance of quietness before the Lord. Our app is named after this call to stillness. We want to provide a digital space that reflects this peace. You can use the app to read or listen to these words at any time of day.

Another passage that guides our work is Psalm 119:105, which says, "Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path." We hope that by providing easy access to the Bible, we can help you keep that light before you. Whether you are starting your morning with a prayer or ending your night with a Psalm, we are honored to be part of your routine. We look forward to sharing this with you soon.
