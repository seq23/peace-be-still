How to cancel a Bible Chat subscription on iPhone or Android

To cancel a Bible Chat subscription, cancel it where you paid: in your iPhone's Settings if you subscribed through the App Store, or in the Google Play app if you subscribed on Android. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. After you cancel, you can normally keep using what you paid for until the end of the current period.

Quick answer

iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Bible Chat › Cancel Subscription. Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Bible Chat › Cancel subscription.

What this page is best for: anyone who subscribed to Bible Chat and wants to stop the next charge.

How do I cancel on an iPhone or iPad?

These are Apple's own steps (Apple Support, “Cancel a subscription from Apple”):

  1. In Settings, tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap the subscription (Bible Chat).
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription. You might need to scroll down to find it.
  5. If there is no Cancel button, or you see an expiration message in red text, the subscription is already cancelled.

How do I cancel on Android?

These are Google's own steps (Google Play Help, “Cancel, pause, or change a subscription on Google Play”):

  1. On your Android device, go to subscriptions in Google Play.
  2. Select the subscription you want to cancel.
  3. Tap Cancel subscription.
  4. Follow the instructions.

Google notes that when you uninstall the app, your subscription won't cancel.

Does deleting the app cancel the subscription?

No. The subscription is billed by Apple or Google, not by the app on your phone, so it keeps renewing until you cancel it in Settings (iPhone) or Google Play (Android).

What if I am on a free trial?

Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, as Apple advises, so it does not turn into a paid plan. You can usually keep using the trial until its end date after cancelling.

What else do people ask?

Why can't I find Bible Chat in my subscriptions?

Check that you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe. If you subscribed on the app's website instead of in the store, you need to cancel it there. A missing Cancel button on iPhone means it is already cancelled.

Will I get a refund if I cancel?

Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund the current period by itself. On iPhone you can ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, Google Play's refund rules apply.

Does cancelling delete my account?

No. Cancelling only stops future charges. Your account and anything saved in the app are separate; the app's own settings or support page explain how to delete an account.
