How to cancel a Bible app subscription: 7 apps, step by step

To cancel a Bible or prayer app subscription, cancel it with whoever bills you: Apple (iPhone Settings), Google Play, or the app's own website. Deleting the app never cancels it. Most charges after “cancelling” come from cancelling in the wrong place, so find the billing source first, then follow the steps below.

Quick answer

Paid in the App Store: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › the app › Cancel Subscription. Paid in Google Play: Play Store › profile icon › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Cancel. Paid on the app's website: cancel in your account on that website. Then check for the red expiry date or a confirmation email.

What this page is best for: anyone trying to stop a Bible, prayer or Christian meditation app from charging again, and anyone who was charged after they thought they had cancelled.

First: where are you being billed?

One app can bill you in up to four places, and only that place can cancel it. Look at the receipt email or your card statement:

  • “Apple” or “APPLE.COM/BILL”: cancel on your iPhone or iPad (steps below).
  • “Google” or “Google Play”: cancel in the Play Store.
  • The app's own name (often through Stripe or PayPal): you signed up on its website; cancel there.
  • Amazon: cancel in your Amazon account's app subscriptions.

If you have more than one Apple ID or Google account, check each one.

How do I cancel on an iPhone or iPad?

Apple's steps (Apple Support, “Cancel a subscription from Apple”):

  1. Open the Settings app and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap the app's subscription.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription. If there is no Cancel button, or you see an expiry date in red, it is already cancelled.

How do I cancel on Android?

Google's steps (Google Play Help):

  1. Open the Google Play Store app and tap your profile icon.
  2. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  3. Choose the app's subscription and tap Cancel subscription.

How to cancel Hallow

Hallow can bill through Apple, Google Play, Amazon or Hallow.com. Its help page says to check first: in the app, go to Me › Settings › Subscription and read the “Subscribed through…” line.

  • Apple, Google Play or Amazon: cancel with that store (steps above). Hallow cannot cancel these for you.
  • Hallow.com, from the app: Me › Settings › Subscription › Cancel My Subscription, tap “Continue to Cancellation” at each prompt, then on the website tap the red Cancel Subscription button and Continue. Wait for “Success! Your Subscription has been canceled and will not renew.”
  • Hallow.com, from a browser: log in at hallow.com, open the menu (three lines, top right) › Settings › Subscription › Cancel Subscription, and confirm.

Hallow says you keep access until the end of the paid period or trial, then return to the free version; to avoid a trial charge, cancel at least 24 hours before it renews. For money back, Hallow says to cancel first, then follow its refund article. Sources: Hallow Help, “How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?” (dated 22 Jul 2026) and “I was charged but want a refund”, both read 21 Nov 2026.

How to cancel Pray.com

Pray.com's help page says to check your bank or card statement first: a charge from “Pray.com” means you bought directly from Pray.com; a charge from Apple or Google means you cancel in that store.

  • Regular Premium bought from Pray.com: log in at pray.com/login with the email used for the subscription, go to Manage Subscription, choose the cancel option and follow the prompts.
  • Give and Go Ad-Free Plan (a donation that comes with an ad-free account): in the Pray.com app, Settings › Manage Donation, then follow the prompts.
  • Apple: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Pray.com › Cancel.
  • Google Play: Google Play Store › Menu › Subscriptions › Pray.com › Cancel.

Pray.com warns not to delete your account before the subscription is cancelled, because deleting it often does not stop billing. If you still cannot find it, its help page asks you to contact support with your account email, the last 4 digits of the card and a screenshot of the charge. Sources: Pray.com Help, “How to Cancel Your Pray.com Subscription” (3 Feb 2026) and “Cancelling a Pray.com trial or subscription from Google”, both read 21 Nov 2026.

How to cancel Glorify

Glorify's help page says to cancel through the store that bills you:

  • iPhone: Settings › your name › Media and Purchases › View Account › Subscriptions › Glorify › Cancel Subscription. (The shorter Settings › your name › Subscriptions path above reaches the same list.)
  • Android: open the Google Play Store, not Glorify › profile icon › Payments & Subscriptions › Subscriptions › Glorify › Cancel subscription.

Glorify notes that deleting your Glorify account does not cancel the subscription, that auto-renew must be off at least 24 hours before the period ends, and that cancelling does not refund what was already paid; you keep premium until it expires. Source: Glorify Help, “How do I cancel my subscription?” (updated 13 Oct 2026, read 21 Nov 2026).

How to cancel Abide

  • App Store: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Abide › Cancel Subscription.
  • Google Play: the Google Play steps above, or in the Abide app, More › Settings › Manage subscription.
  • Abide website (card through Stripe): in the Abide app, More › Settings › Manage Subscription › Cancel.

Abide says uninstalling the app or deleting your account does not cancel, and you keep Premium until the end of the current billing period. Source: Abide Help, “How Do I CANCEL My Premium Subscription?” (updated 6 Oct 2026, read 21 Nov 2026).

How to cancel Dwell

  • Bought on the Dwell website: open your Dwell account page and use the “Billing and Subscription” section, or in the app tap the gear icon (top left) › Subscription and follow the prompts. Dwell's team also offers to cancel a website subscription for you by email.
  • Bought in the App Store or Google Play: cancel with that store (steps above). Dwell says it cannot cancel store subscriptions for you.

Source: Dwell Help, “How do I cancel my Dwell subscription?” (published 21 May 2026, read 21 Nov 2026).

How to cancel Bible Chat

Bible Chat: Daily Devotional (Book Vitals Inc.) offers a free 7-day trial, then a weekly plan at $4.99 or longer plans, according to its App Store listing (checked 21 Nov 2026). The website its listing links to gives the iPhone steps: Settings › your Apple Account › Subscriptions › Bible Chat › Cancel, then confirm (“How to Cancel Your Bible Chat Subscription”, 27 May 2025). On Android, use the Google Play steps above. To stop a trial from turning into a charge, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Full steps: how to cancel a Bible Chat subscription.

How to cancel Haven

Haven - Bible Chat (E12 Holdings LLC) says on its App Store listing that access requires a paid subscription (checked 21 Nov 2026). Its website, havenbibleapp.com, links only to the App Store and Google Play, and its FAQ says you can cancel any time, without listing steps (read 21 Nov 2026). So cancel in the store you subscribed through, with the Apple or Google steps above. There are two apps named Haven, so check the developer name. Full steps: how to cancel a Haven subscription.

Can I get a refund?

  • Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose “Request a refund”, then the reason and the purchase. Apple decides each request.
  • Google Play: Google's refund policy explains when to ask Google and when to ask the developer.
  • Website purchases: ask the app's own support; your bank can help if a charge was never authorised.

Cancelling stops the next renewal; on its own it does not refund the current period.

What if I was charged after I cancelled?

Some App Store reviewers in 2026 report a charge after they believed they had cancelled. In the help pages above, the usual reasons are cancelling in the wrong place (for example deleting the app, or cancelling on the website when Apple bills you), a second account, or a trial cancelled less than 24 hours before it ended. Check each billing source, keep a screenshot of the red expiry date or the confirmation email, then use the refund routes above.

Frequently asked questions

Does deleting a Bible app cancel the subscription?

No. Apple, Google or the app's website keeps billing until you cancel there. Hallow, Glorify, Abide and Pray.com each say this on their own help pages.

Why can't I find the subscription in my iPhone settings?

You may have paid on the app's website, with Google Play, with Amazon, or under another Apple ID. Check the receipt email for who billed you. On iPhone, no Cancel button with a red date means it is already cancelled.

When should I cancel a free trial?

At least 24 hours before it ends. Apple, Hallow and Glorify all give the 24-hour rule. You can usually keep using the trial until its end date.

Will I lose access straight away?

Usually not. Hallow, Glorify, Abide and Apple each say you keep what you paid for until the end of the current period.
