Was Samson Black? What the book of Judges says

The Bible does not say Samson was Black, and it does not say he was not. Judges 13 to 16 tells us he was an Israelite from the tribe of Dan, son of Manoah, a Nazirite whose hair was never cut, worn in seven locks. It never describes his skin.

Quick answer

Samson was an Israelite of the tribe of Dan (Judges 13:2). The seven locks of his hair (Judges 16:13, 19) are why many people picture him with locs today; the verses give the number of locks, not their texture.

What this page is best for: people who have seen Samson drawn with locs and want to know what the text supports.

What does the Bible say about Samson's family?

And there was a certain man of Zorah, of the family of the Danites, whose name was Manoah; and his wife was barren, and bare not.

Judges 13:2 · KJV

He was born to an Israelite couple in Zorah, of the tribe of Dan, after an angel's visit. He was set apart as a Nazirite from birth: no razor on his head.

What were Samson's seven locks?

Delilah weaves the seven locks of his head into a loom, then later has them shaved off:

And she made him sleep upon her knees; and she called for a man, and she caused him to shave off the seven locks of his head; and she began to afflict him, and his strength went from him.

Judges 16:19 · KJV

“Locks” in 1611 English means lengths or tresses of hair. Many Black readers picture these as locs, since uncut hair grown for years and worn in seven sections looks a lot like them. That is a reasonable picture, but the verse does not describe the texture.

Were the Israelites Black?

The Bible does not use colour to describe Israel. The Israelites lived between Africa and Asia, came out of centuries in Egypt, and included people from other nations (Exodus 12:38 speaks of a mixed multitude). Most historians picture ancient Israelites as brown-skinned people of the Near East.

Why does the question matter?

Because for generations, Bible pictures showed every hero as European. Asking “was Samson Black?” is often really asking “can I see myself in this story?”. The answer to that is yes, and the Bible gives no reason to say otherwise.

Frequently asked questions

What tribe was Samson from?

The tribe of Dan. His father Manoah was of the family of the Danites from Zorah (Judges 13:2).

Did Samson have dreadlocks?

Judges 16 says he had seven locks of uncut hair. Many people picture them as locs, and that fits uncut hair worn for years, but the Bible does not describe their texture.

Where is Samson's story in the Bible?

Judges chapters 13 to 16, from the angel's announcement to his parents to his final prayer in the temple of Dagon.
