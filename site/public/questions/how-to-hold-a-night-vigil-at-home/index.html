How to hold a night vigil at home

A home vigil is a night of prayer kept in a living room instead of a church hall. It needs a start time, a simple order that moves between worship, the Word and prayer, someone to lead each part, and an agreed end. Four to six hours is common; even two hours, kept faithfully, is a vigil.

Quick answer

Agree the hours (for example 10 p.m. to 3 a.m.), split the night into blocks of about an hour, give each block a leader and a theme, read one passage aloud in each, and keep water and a short break at the halfway point.

What this page is best for: a family or small fellowship keeping a vigil at home, or anyone who cannot get to the church vigil this month.

Before the night

  • Agree the hours and tell neighbours if you will be singing late.
  • Choose a theme and one passage for each block.
  • Name a leader for each block so no one person carries the whole night.
  • Write the prayer points down. See night vigil prayer points.

Keeping awake without strain

Stand for worship, sit for the Word, and let people walk while they pray. A short break with water at the halfway point helps more than coffee. Nobody should be shamed for dozing; the disciples slept in Gethsemane too.

Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.

Matthew 26:41 · KJV

Closing well

End on time, in thanksgiving, with the blessing. Make sure everyone driving home is rested enough to drive.

The LORD bless thee, and keep thee: The LORD make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee: The LORD lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace.

Numbers 6:24-26 · KJV

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a pastor to hold a vigil?

Many home vigils are led by elders or group leaders. If your church has a house-fellowship structure, let your pastor know and follow its guidance.

Can we hold a vigil on a phone line or video call?

Yes. Many diaspora fellowships pray together on a conference line or video call when members live far apart. Give each block a leader and mute between speakers.

What if only two of us keep it?

Two is enough. Jesus said where two or three are gathered in his name, he is in the midst of them (Matthew 18:20).
