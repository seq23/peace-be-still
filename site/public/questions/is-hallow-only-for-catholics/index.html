Is Hallow only for Catholics?

No, according to Hallow itself: its help page answers “Is Hallow only for Catholics?” with “No.” It also says Hallow is built with Catholic Church leaders so that its content stays faithful to Church teaching. So anyone can use it, but what you will find inside is Catholic prayer: the rosary, the saints, the Catholic Bible.

Quick answer

Hallow welcomes people of every faith, by its own account, and its App Store listing calls it the “#1 Catholic App”. Protestants can use its Scripture readings and Christian meditation, but much of the library is Catholic devotion, so it suits Catholics, people exploring Catholicism, and Christians comfortable with Catholic prayer.

What this page is best for: Protestant, Baptist, Pentecostal, non-denominational and other Christians wondering whether Hallow is meant for them.

What does Hallow say about non-Catholics?

From Hallow's help page (Frequently asked questions, read 21 Nov 2026): to “Is Hallow only for Catholics?” it answers “No.” It says Hallow “is built in close collaboration with leaders of the Catholic Church” to keep content faithful to Church teaching, that it “welcomes people from every faith background”, and that “many non-Catholics use the app as a resource for prayer, meditation, and finding peace.”

Its App Store listing (checked 21 Nov 2026) says the same in other words: content is “based on content from the approved Catholic Bible” and reviewed by senior Church leaders, and “it is meant as a resource for people of all faiths & religions.”

What is inside Hallow that is specifically Catholic?

Its listing names, among other things:

  • The Rosary, “Meditate with Mary through the mysteries of the Catholic Rosary”, and the Divine Mercy Chaplet.
  • Night Prayer from the Liturgy of the Hours, and Catholic Bible stories for sleep.
  • Lectio Divina and the Examen, which are older practices some Protestants also use.
  • Homilies and guests, including Catholic priests.

It also has Scripture readings, Christian meditation and music that many Christians would find familiar.

Does Hallow use a Protestant or a Catholic Bible?

Hallow's listing says its content is based on the approved Catholic Bible. Catholic Bibles include books Protestant Bibles leave out, such as Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach and 1 and 2 Maccabees. The King James Version of 1611 printed these between the testaments as the Apocrypha, and most KJV printings today leave them out.

What do reviewers who are not Catholic say?

Among Hallow's 500 most recent US App Store reviews (24 Mar to 18 Nov 2026, read 21 Nov 2026), a handful mention not being Catholic, and they split. One five-star reviewer called it “Excellent for Catholics & I still reccomend it to non Catholics” (13 Sep 2026). A three-star reviewer wrote that it is “very much an app for Catholics and not for Christians in general” (9 Apr 2026). Others mention exploring Catholicism through it. These are reviewers' views, not ours.
