Night vigil prayer points, each with its verse

Night vigil prayer points are short, specific prayers that a vigil works through one by one through the night, usually from late evening into the early hours. Most vigils move from praise and thanksgiving, through repentance, into intercession for family, church and nation, and end in thanksgiving. Below are 12 points in that order, each standing on a King James verse.

Quick answer

Start with praise (Psalm 119:62), confess before you ask (Psalm 51), pray for your family by name, for your church and pastor, for the nation where you live and the one you came from, then close in thanksgiving.

What this page is best for: anyone leading or joining a vigil who wants prayer points that each rest on Scripture.

Why pray at night?

Scripture is full of night prayer. David rose at midnight to give thanks. Jesus continued all night in prayer before he chose the twelve. Paul and Silas prayed and sang at midnight in prison.

And at midnight Paul and Silas prayed, and sang praises unto God: and the prisoners heard them.

Acts 16:25 · KJV

And it came to pass in those days, that he went out into a mountain to pray, and continued all night in prayer to God.

Luke 6:12 · KJV

Praise and thanksgiving (to open)

  1. Thank God for keeping you and your household through the week, by name.

    At midnight I will rise to give thanks unto thee because of thy righteous judgments.

    Psalm 119:62 · KJV

  2. Praise him for who he is before asking for anything.

    Bless the LORD, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits:

    Psalm 103:2 · KJV

Repentance

  1. Ask the Lord to search your heart, and confess what he shows you.

    Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts: And see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting.

    Psalm 139:23-24 · KJV

  2. Receive his mercy, and forgive anyone you are holding something against.

    If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

    1 John 1:9 · KJV

Family

  1. Pray for your spouse, children and parents by name, here and back home.

    And if it seem evil unto you to serve the LORD, choose you this day whom ye will serve; whether the gods which your fathers served that were on the other side of the flood, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land ye dwell: but as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD.

    Joshua 24:15 · KJV

  2. Pray for the children to know the Lord for themselves.

    And all thy children shall be taught of the LORD; and great shall be the peace of thy children.

    Isaiah 54:13 · KJV

Church and pastor

  1. Pray for your pastor, the ministers and the workers, that they stand firm.

    Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints;

    Ephesians 6:18 · KJV

  2. Pray for unity in the fellowship.

    Endeavouring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.

    Ephesians 4:3 · KJV

The nations

  1. Pray for the country where you live: its cities, its leaders, your neighbours and your workplace.

    And seek the peace of the city whither I have caused you to be carried away captives, and pray unto the LORD for it: for in the peace thereof shall ye have peace.

    Jeremiah 29:7 · KJV

  2. Pray for the country you came from: peace, honest leaders, and the church there.

    I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men; For kings, and for all that are in authority; that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty.

    1 Timothy 2:1-2 · KJV

Thanksgiving (to close)

  1. Thank God for hearing, before you see any answer.

    Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

    Philippians 4:6-7 · KJV

  2. Commit the coming week into his hands.

    Commit thy way unto the LORD; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass.

    Psalm 37:5 · KJV

Frequently asked questions

What is a night vigil?

A night vigil is a prayer meeting that runs through the night, often from about 10 or 11 p.m. into the early hours, with worship, the Word and long seasons of prayer. Many African churches in the US hold vigils regularly, often on a Friday night.

How many prayer points should a vigil have?

There is no rule. Many leaders use 7 to 12, giving each one several minutes, rather than racing through 50.

What is the difference between midnight prayer and a vigil?

Midnight prayer is a single time of prayer at or around midnight; a vigil keeps watch through the night. The same prayer points work for both.
