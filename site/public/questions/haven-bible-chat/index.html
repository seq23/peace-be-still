Haven Bible Chat: what it is, and what else to consider

Haven - Bible Chat is an iPhone app by E12 Holdings LLC with daily verses, devotionals, prayers, a streak and a chat you can “ask Haven anything”. Its listing says access requires a paid subscription, shows a 12+ age rating and English only (checked 18 Nov 2026). Alternatives include Bible chat apps with a free version, such as Peace Be Still, on the App Store and Google Play.
