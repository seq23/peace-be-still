Night prayer: how to end the day with God

A night prayer is the prayer that closes the day before God: thanks for what was good, honesty about what went wrong, and handing over what is still unfinished. A short one for tonight: “Lord, thank you for today. Forgive me where I failed. I give you what I could not finish. Keep my house tonight. Amen.”

Quick answer

Thank, confess, hand over, then rest on one verse such as Psalm 4:8. Five minutes is enough. If you wake in the night, pray one line and a verse, and lie back down without picking up the phone.

What this page is best for: anyone who wants a simple way to end the day in prayer, and anyone awake at 3 a.m. who wants something to pray.

What is a good night prayer?

One you will actually pray. This one takes under a minute:

“Lord, thank you for this day, for what went well and for what the hard parts taught me. Forgive me where I failed you and the people around me. I give you what I could not finish and what I could not fix. Watch over the ones I love tonight, near and far. Let me lie down in peace. Amen.”

I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety.

Psalm 4:8 · KJV

How do you pray at night?

  1. Look back for one minute. Where did you see God today?
  2. Give thanks for two things, by name.
  3. Confess what went wrong, plainly, without replaying it.
  4. Hand over what is unfinished. It will still be there in the morning.
  5. End on a verse and turn off the light.

Yet the LORD will command his lovingkindness in the daytime, and in the night his song shall be with me, and my prayer unto the God of my life.

Psalm 42:8 · KJV

What should I pray if I wake up at 3 a.m.?

Keep it short, so prayer does not turn into a worry list. Many people pray one line and a verse:

“Lord, you are awake when I am awake. I give you what woke me. Watch over me until morning. Amen.”

When I remember thee upon my bed, and meditate on thee in the night watches.

Psalm 63:6 · KJV

Then lie back down. If something specific woke you, write it on paper by the bed and leave it there until morning. If waking in the night happens often, it is worth mentioning to your doctor.

Is there midnight prayer in the Bible?

Yes. David wrote At midnight I will rise to give thanks unto thee, and Jesus continued all night in prayer to God. In prison at Philippi, Paul and Silas prayed and sang at midnight:

And at midnight Paul and Silas prayed, and sang praises unto God: and the prisoners heard them.

Acts 16:25 · KJV

Which psalms are prayed at night?

Psalm 4 is David's evening prayer and ends with the verse above. Psalm 91 asks for protection (Thou shalt not be afraid for the terror by night). Psalm 121 is the keeper who never sleeps, and Psalm 134 blesses those which by night stand in the house of the LORD. In Anglican and Catholic churches, Night Prayer (also called Compline) is the last set prayer of the day and uses psalms like these.

Frequently asked questions

What is the night prayer called?

In many homes it is simply the evening or bedtime prayer. In Anglican and Catholic churches the set night prayer is called Compline, or Night Prayer. Any honest prayer at the end of the day counts; you do not need a set form to pray at night.

Is it wrong to fall asleep while praying?

No. Falling asleep while talking to God is not a failure. Psalm 4 ends with lying down in peace and sleeping, and that is a good place to stop. Pray what matters first, then rest.

Why do people pray at 3 a.m.?

Some wake then and use the time to pray; some churches keep night watches. Scripture shows prayer in the night watches (Psalm 63:6) and at midnight (Acts 16:25). Keep it short and go back to sleep when you can.

How long should a night prayer be?

A few minutes. A long night prayer often becomes a list of worries. Say what matters, give the rest to God, and close with one verse you can carry to sleep.
