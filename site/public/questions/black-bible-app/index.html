A Bible app for Black believers: what to look for

A Black Bible app is a Bible app made with Black believers in mind: the same scripture, but with a voice that sounds like the Black church, Bible stories drawn with Black faces and names, and room for the songs and history that shaped that faith. People search for it because most Bible apps' art and voices rarely look or sound like them.

Quick answer

Look for three things: scripture quoted word for word from a named translation, a voice and art that feel like home, and clear prices with no surprise charges. Peace Be Still's Hush Harbor journey is built around all three and is coming soon.

What this page is best for: Black Christians, and the people who love them, looking for a Bible app that feels like their own church.

What do people mean by a Black Bible app?

Searches like “African American Bible app”, “Bible app for Black women” and “best Bible app for Black people” come from the same wish: to read and talk about scripture in a space that reflects your family, your church and your history. The Bible is the same; the setting, the faces and the voice are what change.

What should a Bible app for Black believers have?

  • Exact scripture. Every verse quoted word for word from a named translation, with book, chapter and verse. An AI should never write a verse from memory.
  • A voice that feels like home. Warm, plain, church-family language, without stereotypes.
  • Faces and names that look like you. Bible Stories with Black Faces, faithful to the text.
  • History told truthfully. Hush harbors were the hidden places where enslaved people met to worship in secret; that history belongs in the story.
  • Honest prices. The price in dollars before you tap, no trial that turns into a charge, and an easy way to cancel.

What is Hush Harbor in Peace Be Still?

Hush Harbor · Black Bible Chat & Stories is a journey inside Peace Be Still, a phone app coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. You talk through what is on your heart in a Black church voice, and the answer comes back with real King James verses. Frequently asked questions

Is there a Bible app made for Black people?

Peace Be Still, coming soon to the App Store and Google Play, has a journey called Hush Harbor · Black Bible Chat & Stories: a chat in a Black church voice that answers with exact King James verses, and a daily Bible story drawn with Black faces and names.

What is a hush harbor?

Hush harbors were hidden places, often in woods or swamps, where enslaved African Americans gathered to worship, pray and sing away from those who enslaved them. The journey takes its name from that history of faith kept alive in secret.

Which Bible translation does Hush Harbor use?

The King James Version, quoted word for word from a stored copy with book, chapter and verse. The AI chooses which verses to show, but the words always come from the stored text, never from the AI's memory.

Is Hush Harbor only for Black users?

No. Everyone is welcome in every journey. Hush Harbor is made with Black believers in mind, in its voice, its art and its stories, and the rest of Peace Be Still is open to everyone.
