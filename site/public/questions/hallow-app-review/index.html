Hallow app review: who it suits, and what to weigh before paying

Hallow is a Catholic prayer and meditation app with audio-guided sessions: the rosary, Lectio Divina, the Examen, Night Prayer, chant and Bible stories read by guests. What App Store reviewers report (Nov 2026)

Among Hallow's 500 most recent US App Store reviews (24 Mar to 18 Nov 2026), 370 gave 4 or 5 stars and 103 gave 1 or 2. About half of those 103 (51) mention cancelling, refunds or being charged, most often a charge after a trial, or after the reviewer believed they had cancelled. Some low ratings object to paying for prayer at all.

How do I cancel an App Store subscription?

  1. iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › the app › Cancel Subscription.
  2. Android: Google Play › profile icon › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › the app › Cancel subscription.
