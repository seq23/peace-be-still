A Daily Devotional for the Black Woman

A daily devotional for the Black woman is a dedicated time of prayer and scripture reading to center the spirit. It is a practice of opening the Word of God each morning or evening. This routine provides a steady foundation through the King James Version and stories that reflect her own image.

Published 19 November 2026 · Peace Be Still

How do I start a daily practice with the Word?

A daily practice begins with a quiet heart and an open Bible. You might choose to sit on your porch or find a soft chair before the sun rises. Reading the Word of God provides a lamp unto your feet. It is a time to set aside the noise of the world and listen to the ancient truths found in the King James Version. Many women find comfort in starting with the Psalms. These verses speak to the soul in every season of life. You can read them slowly or listen to them whispered as you prepare for the day. A devotional is not a task to finish but a well to draw from. It is a moment to breathe and remember the promises written long ago. This practice becomes a steady anchor for your household and your peace.

The Peace Be Still app is designed to support this rhythm. It offers the whole King James Bible to read, and a growing set of chapters read aloud. You can find a daily Bible story to guide your thoughts. For those who speak Spanish, Mi Refugio provides the Reina-Valera 1909. This ensures the Word is accessible in the language of your heart. There are no ads to distract you from your prayer. There are no surprise charges or trials to track. It is a simple space for a sacred habit. You can return to it every morning and every night without interruption.

What is the Hush Harbor in Peace Be Still?

The Hush Harbor is a special place within the app for reflection and storytelling. It features Bible stories with Black faces. Seeing the people of the Bible reflected in your own image is a powerful experience. It reminds the reader that the Word of God is for everyone. These stories are told with care and reverence. They are perfect for a mother to share with her children or for a woman to enjoy in her own quiet time. The Hush Harbor honors the tradition of finding a safe space to worship and learn. It is a digital sanctuary where the stories of old come to life through beautiful illustrations and faithful narration.

Each story stays true to the scripture. You will find the accounts of faith, courage, and grace that have sustained generations. The Hush Harbor is part of the daily Bible story offering. It helps make the devotional time feel personal and welcoming. You can spend time here whenever you need to see the beauty of the Word in a way that feels like home. It is a place of rest and recognition for the Black woman seeking a deeper connection to her faith.

Can I listen to scripture at bedtime?

Bedtime is a natural season for the Word. Many people find that listening to the Bible helps them settle their thoughts after a long day. Peace Be Still offers Psalm Nights for this very purpose. These are whispered readings of the Psalms. The voice is soft and unhurried. It is like a grandmother reading to you by the light of a lamp. You can set a sleep timer so the reading ends on its own. This allows you to focus on the words of the King James Version as you close your eyes. The scripture remains the focus of the night.

The Bible says in Psalm 4:8, "I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, Lord, only makest me dwell in safety." This verse from the King James Version reminds us where our safety lies. A nightly practice of hearing the Word can be a gentle way to end the day. There are no loud sounds or bright ads to wake you. The app is designed to be dark and quiet. It respects your need for a peaceful environment as you prepare for rest. You can choose different Psalms each night to keep your practice fresh and meaningful.

How does the Bible chat work?

Sometimes you have a question about a verse or need a specific prayer. The app includes a Bible chat feature. This tool is AI and is clearly labelled as AI. It is designed to help you find scripture quickly. When you ask a question, it responds by quoting King James Version verses word for word. It does not paraphrase the holy text. This ensures that you are always receiving the actual Word of God. It is a helpful companion for your daily devotional when you want to explore a specific topic like patience or strength.

The chat is there to support your study, not to replace your own reading. It can point you toward a Psalm or a story in the Hush Harbor. For Spanish speakers, the chat in Mi Refugio uses the Reina-Valera 1909. This makes it easy to find the right words in your preferred translation. It is a simple way to interact with the Bible throughout the day. Whether you are looking for a morning declaration or a verse for a friend, the chat provides a direct path to the scriptures you need.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a version of the app for Spanish speakers?

Yes, the app includes Mi Refugio. This section offers the Biblia y chat en español using the Reina-Valera 1909 translation. It provides the same quiet experience and whispered readings for Spanish-speaking families to enjoy during their daily devotional time.

What Bible translation is used in the app?

Peace Be Still uses the King James Version (KJV) for all English scripture. Every verse quoted in the chat or read in the audio sections is taken word for word from this historic translation. For Spanish, the app uses the Reina-Valera 1909.

Are there any hidden fees or trials?

There are no trials and no surprise charges. The app has a free lane that stays free forever. For those who want more features, there is a paid lane with a clear monthly or yearly price. You will never see an ad in the app.

What are Bible stories with Black faces?

These are illustrated stories found in the Hush Harbor section of the app. They depict biblical figures as people of color. This allows Black women and their families to see themselves reflected in the sacred stories of the Bible while staying true to the scripture.
