Finding a quiet place to read

Walking with the Word

"Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path." (Psalm 119:105). This is the foundation of our work. We do not offer quick fixes or magic words. We offer the text of the Bible. You can read it or hear it. The whispered readings are meant to help you focus on the scripture. The Bible stories told in a Black church voice help you see yourself in the history of the faith. It is a simple, unhurried way to live out your devotion.
