How to cancel the “I am - Daily Affirmations” subscription

“I am - Daily Affirmations”, by Monkey Taps, sells its Premium through the App Store and Google Play, so you cancel it in your phone's store subscriptions, not inside I am itself. Removing it from your phone does not cancel the subscription. A lifetime purchase does not renew, so there is nothing to cancel.

Quick answer

iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › I am › Cancel Subscription. Android: Google Play › profile icon › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › I am › Cancel subscription.

What this page is best for: anyone who wants to stop the next charge from the I am affirmations app.

What the listing says

Its US App Store listing (checked 21 Nov 2026) names the seller as Monkey Taps and offers I am Premium as a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription, or a lifetime purchase. We found no cancel page on the developer's website, so the store steps below are the official route.

Cancel on iPhone or iPad

Apple's steps (Apple Support, “Cancel a subscription from Apple”):

  1. Open Settings and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap the subscription (I am).
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription. You might need to scroll down.
  5. If there is no Cancel button, or an expiry date shows in red, it is already cancelled.

Cancel on Android

Google's steps (Google Play Help): open the Play Store, tap your profile icon › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, choose the subscription and tap Cancel subscription, then follow the instructions. If you do not see it, check you are signed in to the Google account you paid with.

Frequently asked questions

Does deleting the I am app cancel my subscription?

No. Cancel it in your Apple or Google Play subscriptions.

I bought lifetime. Do I need to cancel?

No. A lifetime purchase is a one-time payment and does not renew.

Can I get a refund?

For App Store purchases, request one through Apple's Report a Problem page; for Google Play, through Google Play's refund request.
