Is my AI chat private, or used for training?

An AI chat is private only if the company does not keep your conversations longer than needed and does not use them to train or improve its models. Many consumer chat apps do use chats for training by default unless you turn a setting off, and free AI services often say people may review what you type.

Quick answer

Check the chat's data or privacy settings for a training switch, read the privacy policy for “train” and “retain”, and use a no-history or temporary mode for anything sensitive. In ChatGPT, OpenAI says personal plans share data by default; turn off “Improve the model for everyone” in Settings › Data controls (read 21 Nov 2026).

What this page is best for: anyone who talks to an AI chat about personal things.

Three questions to ask of any AI chat

  1. Training: may my chats be used to improve the model, and is that on by default?
  2. Keeping: how long are chats kept, and can people at the company read them?
  3. Control: is there a setting or a mode that turns this off?

ChatGPT's own settings

From OpenAI's Data Controls FAQ (read 21 Nov 2026):

  • On ChatGPT Free, Plus and Pro, data sharing to improve the model is on by default; Business, Enterprise and Edu workspaces are excluded by default.
  • To turn it off on the web: account menu › Settings › Data controls › switch off “Improve the model for everyone”. On iPhone and Android: profile icon › Settings › Data controls, same switch.
  • Temporary Chats do not appear in your history, do not create memories and are not used to improve models; OpenAI says they may be kept for up to 30 days for safety.

A practical rule

Leave out names, addresses and anything you would not want a stranger to read in a free AI chat, unless its policy says plainly that chats are not kept and not used for training.

What else do people ask?

Does ChatGPT use my chats for training?

On personal plans, by default, yes, according to OpenAI's Data Controls FAQ (read 21 Nov 2026). You can switch it off in Settings › Data controls.

Is a temporary chat private?

In ChatGPT, OpenAI says Temporary Chats are not used to improve models but may be kept for up to 30 days for safety.

Are free AI chats less private?

Often. Free services commonly say they may keep and use what you type. Check the policy of the specific service.
