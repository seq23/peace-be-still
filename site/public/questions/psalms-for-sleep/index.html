Psalms for sleep: 6 bedtime psalms to read or hear tonight

Psalms for sleep are psalms people read or listen to at bedtime because they speak of rest, safety and trust in God. The most used are Psalm 4, 3, 23, 91, 121 and 127. Many people play one psalm read slowly, or whispered, with a sleep timer, so scripture is the last thing they hear before sleeping.

Quick answer

Start with Psalm 4, the traditional evening psalm, which ends with lying down in peace. Then try Psalm 23 or Psalm 91. Read or listen to one psalm, slowly, with the lights low.

What this page is best for: people who want a short list of bedtime psalms, quoted exactly, and a simple way to use them.

Which Psalm is for sleeping in peace?

Psalm 4. It is an evening prayer: David asks to be heard, tells the reader to commune with your own heart upon your bed, and be still, and ends with lying down in peace. Here it is in full, eight verses, about two minutes read slowly:

  1. To the chief Musician on Neginoth, A Psalm of David. Hear me when I call, O God of my righteousness: thou hast enlarged me when I was in distress; have mercy upon me, and hear my prayer.
  2. O ye sons of men, how long will ye turn my glory into shame? how long will ye love vanity, and seek after leasing? Selah.
  3. But know that the LORD hath set apart him that is godly for himself: the LORD will hear when I call unto him.
  4. Stand in awe, and sin not: commune with your own heart upon your bed, and be still. Selah.
  5. Offer the sacrifices of righteousness, and put your trust in the LORD.
  6. There be many that say, Who will shew us any good? LORD, lift thou up the light of thy countenance upon us.
  7. Thou hast put gladness in my heart, more than in the time that their corn and their wine increased.
  8. I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety.

Psalm 4:1-8 · KJV

What does the Bible say about sleep?

That it is a gift, not a reward for finishing everything. Psalm 127:2 says worrying late does not add anything, for so he giveth his beloved sleep. Psalm 121 says the one who keeps you shall neither slumber nor sleep, so you can.

Is it okay to fall asleep listening to the Bible?

Yes. Psalm 91 sleep readings on YouTube have gathered millions of views, and many people fall asleep before the reading ends. Set a sleep timer so the audio fades out, and turn off autoplay so nothing new starts.

What is a short prayer to say before bed?

Pray Psalm 4:8 back to God in your own words: “Lord, I am lying down now. I am trusting you to keep me safe tonight.” For more, see prayers for sleep.
