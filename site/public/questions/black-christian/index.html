Black Christian: devotionals, church and Bible tools made with you in mind

A Black Christian is a follower of Jesus whose faith is often shaped by the Black church: its preaching, its songs and its history of faith under slavery and segregation. Pew Research found 66% of Black Americans are Protestant, and 60% of Black churchgoers attend congregations where most people and the senior clergy are Black.

Quick answer

For devotionals that fit, look for exact scripture in a translation you know, a voice that sounds like home, and stories that show people who look like you. Peace Be Still's Hush Harbor is built on those three things.

What this page is best for: Black believers looking for daily tools, and leaders planning devotionals for their church.

What is the Black church?

The phrase covers the historically Black denominations (such as the African Methodist Episcopal Church, founded in Philadelphia in 1816) and the many Black congregations in other traditions. Pew Research's 2021 survey of 8,660 Black adults found:

  • 66% of Black Americans are Protestant, 6% Catholic and 3% another Christian faith.
  • 60% of Black adults who attend services go to congregations where most attendees and the senior clergy are Black.
  • Most Black adults say they rely on prayer to help make major decisions.

Traditions many Black Christians share

  • Watch Night on New Year's Eve, linked by the Smithsonian's National Museum of African American History and Culture to 31 December 1862, when Black communities waited for the Emancipation Proclamation to take effect.
  • Call and response: “God is good.” “All the time.”
  • The spirituals, such as “Go Down, Moses” and “Wade in the Water”.
  • Church mothers, whose sayings carry as much weight as many sermons.

Where do Black Christians find devotionals made for them?

Start with your own church's reading plan. Beyond that, look for three things in any book or app: scripture quoted exactly from a named translation, a voice that does not feel borrowed, and honesty about history. Avoid anything that promises money or healing for a donation.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage of Black Americans are Christian?

Pew Research's 2021 report found 66% are Protestant, 6% Catholic and 3% belong to other Christian faiths: about three in four in all.

What is Watch Night?

A New Year's Eve service. The Smithsonian's NMAAHC ties it to 31 December 1862, “Freedom's Eve”, when Black communities gathered to wait for the Emancipation Proclamation.

What is the oldest Black denomination in the US?

The African Methodist Episcopal Church, founded by Richard Allen and others in Philadelphia in 1816, is the oldest independent Black denomination.
