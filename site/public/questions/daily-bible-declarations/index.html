Daily Bible declarations: how to say scripture out loud each morning

A daily Bible declaration is a short sentence you say out loud in the morning that agrees with something God's Word already says, with the verse beside it. For example, Psalm 118:24 says This is the day which the LORD hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it. The declaration is: “This is the day the Lord has made, and I will rejoice in it.” It is speaking scripture, not inventing promises.

Quick answer

Take one verse, keep its meaning, put it in the first person, and say it out loud once in the morning. Keep the verse reference next to it so you never drift from what the text says.

What this page is best for: people who want to start the day with scripture in their own mouth, rooted in exact verses.

What is a declaration in the Bible?

To declare is to say something openly. The Psalms are full of people saying God's works out loud: I will declare thy name unto my brethren (Psalm 22:22). A daily declaration follows that pattern: you say aloud what scripture says about God and about how to live.

I shall not die, but live, and declare the works of the LORD.

Psalm 118:17 · KJV

How do I make a declaration from scripture?

  1. Pick one verse and read it in context, so you know what it means.
  2. Keep the promise the verse makes, and no more. If it is about God's presence, do not turn it into a promise of money.
  3. Say it in the first person, with the reference: “The Lord is my shepherd, so I have what I need today.” (from Psalm 23:1)

What should I declare over my day?

Things scripture actually says: that God is with you, that his mercy is new, that you can be still, that you will give thanks. Be wary of declarations that promise a result the verse never promised. A declaration is agreement with God's Word, not a way to make God do something.

Frequently asked questions

Are declarations biblical?

Saying God's Word out loud is all through scripture: the Psalms declare his works, and Romans 10:10 says with the mouth confession is made. A declaration is biblical when it stays with what the verse says.

What is the difference between a declaration and a prayer?

A prayer speaks to God. A declaration speaks God's Word out loud, often to yourself, as a reminder of what is true. Many people do both: declare the verse, then pray about the day.

Should I say declarations out loud?

Yes, if you can. Hearing your own voice say a verse helps it stay with you. A whisper in a shared room works too.

Can I make up my own declaration?

Make it from a verse, keep the verse's meaning, and put the reference beside it. That keeps it rooted in scripture rather than in what you hope will happen.
