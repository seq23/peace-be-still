Is the Creed Bible app free?

Creed: Bible Chat & Companion is free to download with limited use. Its App Store description says free users get limited access to AI chat and voice features and need a subscription for unlimited access. Its In-App Purchases list prices from $9.99 to $69.99, including “Annual - Discounted” at $34.99 and $44.99 (US, checked 18 Nov 2026).
