Bible verses for sleep: 10 to read before bed

The Bible verses most read before sleep are Psalm 4:8, Proverbs 3:24, Psalm 127:2, Psalm 3:5 and Psalm 121:3-4. They are verses that actually mention lying down or sleeping. The best known is Psalm 4:8: “I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety.”

Quick answer

Tonight, read Proverbs 3:24 once, then Psalm 4:8 twice, slowly, and turn the light off. Keep the same two verses for a week.

What this page is best for: people who want verses that are about sleep itself, quoted exactly, not a long general list.

What does the Bible say about sleep?

It names sleep as something God gives (Psalm 127:2), something you can do even when life is not settled (Psalm 3:5), and a picture of trust: the One who keeps you does not sleep, so you can. It also shows Jesus himself asleep on a cushion in a boat during a storm, which is where the name of this app comes from.

And he arose, and rebuked the wind, and said unto the sea, Peace, be still. And the wind ceased, and there was a great calm.

Which one should I read tonight?

  • If today was heavy: Matthew 11:28.
  • If you are afraid: Psalm 91:5 and Psalm 4:8.
  • If your mind keeps running: Isaiah 26:3.
  • If you woke at 3 a.m.: Psalm 63:6.

Is it okay to fall asleep listening to the Bible?

Yes. Many people play a psalm or a short passage read softly and fall asleep before it ends. Use a sleep timer so it stops on its own, keep the screen dark, and make sure nothing new starts playing.

What else do people ask?

What is the best Bible verse for sleep?

Psalm 4:8 is the verse most people choose: I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety. Proverbs 3:24 is a close second because it says thy sleep shall be sweet.

Where in the Bible does it say God gives his beloved sleep?

Psalm 127:2: It is vain for you to rise up early, to sit up late, to eat the bread of sorrows: for so he giveth his beloved sleep.

Did Jesus sleep in the Bible?

Yes. Mark 4:38 says he was in the hinder part of the ship, asleep on a pillow during a storm. When the disciples woke him, he calmed the wind and the sea.
