A Bedtime Prayer for Adults

A bedtime prayer for adults is a quiet nightly practice of turning your heart toward God before sleep. It is a moment to quiet the day, seek His peace, and rest in His word. You can read or listen to scripture without rushing.

Published 20 November 2026 · Peace Be Still

Why adults need a quiet prayer before sleep

The house finally grows still. Your mind keeps running through tomorrow's tasks, family needs, and old worries. A simple bedtime prayer for adults helps you lay those burdens down at the feet of the Lord. You do not need grand words or long speeches. He already knows your heart before you speak.

Whispering a short prayer shifts your focus from your own strength to His great mercy. The Bible tells us how to rest without fear. Psalm 4:8 says, I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, Lord, only makest me dwell in safety. You can let go of the day's weight and trust Him to watch over your home through the dark hours.

  • Lay down daily worries in quiet prayer.
  • Turn your thoughts from tasks to God's grace.
  • Rest safely under the shadow of His wings.
  • Find calm after a long and weary day.
