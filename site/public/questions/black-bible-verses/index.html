Bible verses about Black skin, Africa and freedom

Bible verses that mention Black or dark skin include Song of Solomon 1:5, I am black, but comely, and Jeremiah 13:23, about the Ethiopian's skin. Verses about Africa include Psalm 68:31 and Amos 9:7. Black churches also hold close freedom verses such as Exodus 3:7 and Isaiah 61:1.

Quick answer

Skin: Song of Solomon 1:5-6, Jeremiah 13:23. Africa: Psalm 68:31, Amos 9:7, Acts 8:27. Freedom: Exodus 3:7, Isaiah 61:1 (read by Jesus in Luke 4), Galatians 3:28.

What this page is best for: anyone looking for scripture that speaks to Black identity, quoted exactly.

Which Bible verses mention dark skin?

I am black, but comely, O ye daughters of Jerusalem, as the tents of Kedar, as the curtains of Solomon. Look not upon me, because I am black, because the sun hath looked upon me: my mother’s children were angry with me; they made me the keeper of the vineyards; but mine own vineyard have I not kept.

Song of Solomon 1:5-6 · KJV

The woman in the Song is dark from working in the sun, and she says it with her head up: black, and lovely.

Can the Ethiopian change his skin, or the leopard his spots? then may ye also do good, that are accustomed to do evil.

Jeremiah 13:23 · KJV

Verses about Africa and Ethiopia

Princes shall come out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall soon stretch out her hands unto God.

Psalm 68:31 · KJV

Are ye not as children of the Ethiopians unto me, O children of Israel? saith the LORD. Have not I brought up Israel out of the land of Egypt? and the Philistines from Caphtor, and the Syrians from Kir?

Amos 9:7 · KJV

In Amos, God tells Israel that he cares for the Ethiopians as he does for them. It is one of the plainest verses about God and all peoples.

Freedom verses the Black church loves

And the LORD said, I have surely seen the affliction of my people which are in Egypt, and have heard their cry by reason of their taskmasters; for I know their sorrows;

Exodus 3:7 · KJV

The Spirit of the Lord GOD is upon me; because the LORD hath anointed me to preach good tidings unto the meek; he hath sent me to bind up the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives, and the opening of the prison to them that are bound;

Isaiah 61:1 · KJV

Jesus read this passage aloud in the synagogue at Nazareth and said it was fulfilled that day (Luke 4:16-21).

Frequently asked questions

Where does the Bible say “I am black, but comely”?

Song of Solomon 1:5 (I am black, but comely). The speaker is a young woman darkened by the sun from working in the vineyards (1:6). Some newer translations say “dark and lovely”.

Is there a verse about Ethiopia stretching out her hands?

Yes, Psalm 68:31: Princes shall come out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall soon stretch out her hands unto God. Black preachers have quoted it for generations.

What verse says God made all nations of one blood?

Acts 17:26, from Paul's speech in Athens: God hath made of one blood all nations of men for to dwell on all the face of the earth.
