Bible verses for sleepless nights are passages from the Holy Scriptures that you read or hear in the dark to quiet your racing thoughts and anchor your heart in God's peace. A nightly practice of scripture helps your mind let go of the day before you rest.

What does the Bible say about quiet rest?

When the night is long and sleep stays away, your bed can feel like a lonely place. The quiet hours often bring worries that hid in the sun. God knows every toss and turn. He does not sleep. He watches over you in the dark just as He does in the day.

The King James Version gives us words to lean on when your eyes remain open. You can turn to the Psalms when the house is still. Listen to a whispered voice read the ancient verses that have comforted generations of believers before you.

Psalm 4:8 says: "I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, Lord, only makest me dwell in safety." This is a quiet promise to hold in your heart. You are safe in your room. The Lord is keeping watch while you close your eyes.
