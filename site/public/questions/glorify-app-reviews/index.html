What App Store reviewers report (Nov 2026)

Among Glorify's 500 most recent US App Store reviews (15 Nov 2025 to 18 Nov 2026), 440 gave 4 or 5 stars and 41 gave 1 or 2. The most common theme in those 41 is audio: reviewers say listening to the devotionals and passages used to be free and now needs Glorify Plus, and several who listen because of dyslexia or ADHD describe it as an access problem. Four mention the Glorify Ring advertisement shown in the app in July 2026.
