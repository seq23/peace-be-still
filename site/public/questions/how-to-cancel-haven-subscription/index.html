How to cancel a Haven subscription on iPhone or Android

To cancel a Haven subscription, cancel it with the store that bills you: in your iPhone's Settings under Subscriptions if you paid through the App Store, or in the Google Play app if you paid on Android. Uninstalling Haven does not cancel it. Once cancelled, it stops renewing at the end of the period you have already paid for.

Quick answer

iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Haven › Cancel Subscription. Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Haven › Cancel subscription.

What this page is best for: anyone who subscribed to Haven in the App Store or Google Play and wants to stop the next charge.

How do I cancel Haven on an iPhone or iPad?

Apple's own steps (Apple Support, “Cancel a subscription from Apple”):

  1. In Settings, tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap the subscription (Haven).
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription. You might need to scroll down to find it.
  5. If there is no Cancel button, or you see an expiration message in red text, the subscription is already cancelled.

How do I cancel Haven on Android?

Google's own steps (Google Play Help):

  1. On your Android device, go to subscriptions in Google Play.
  2. Select the subscription you want to cancel.
  3. Tap Cancel subscription.
  4. Follow the instructions.

Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

What happens after I cancel?

The subscription stops renewing. You can normally keep using the paid features until the end of the period you already paid for. Cancelling does not delete your account or refund the current period.

Is there a way to cancel on Haven's website?

No steps are listed there. Haven's website, havenbibleapp.com (E12 Holdings), links only to the App Store and Google Play, and its FAQ says you can cancel any time (read 21 Nov 2026). Cancel in the store you subscribed through, with the steps above. Haven's App Store listing says “Access to Haven requires a paid subscription” (checked 21 Nov 2026).

Can I get a refund?

For App Store purchases, you can ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com; Apple decides. For Google Play purchases, Google Play's refund policy applies.

What else do people ask?

Why don't I see Haven under Subscriptions?

Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe. If you paid on a website rather than in the store, cancel it on that website. On iPhone, no Cancel button means it is already cancelled.

Does deleting Haven stop the charges?

No. The App Store or Google Play bills the subscription, so it keeps renewing until you cancel it in iPhone Settings or in Google Play. Deleting the app only removes it from your phone.

When should I cancel a free trial?

Apple advises cancelling at least 24 hours before a trial ends so it does not renew as a paid subscription. You can usually keep using the trial until its end date after cancelling.
