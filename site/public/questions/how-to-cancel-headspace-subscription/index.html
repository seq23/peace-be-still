How to cancel a Headspace subscription, step by step

To cancel Headspace, cancel where you subscribed: on the Headspace website in a browser (not in Headspace's phone app) if you paid Headspace directly, in iPhone Settings if you paid through Apple, or in the Google Play Store if you paid on Android. Removing Headspace from your phone does not cancel it.

Quick answer

Website: log in at headspace.com in a browser, go to headspace.com/subscription/manage, tap Cancel Membership. iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Headspace › Cancel Subscription. Android: Google Play › Menu › Subscriptions › Headspace › Cancel subscription.

What this page is best for: anyone who wants to stop the next Headspace charge.

Headspace's steps

From Headspace's help page, “How do I cancel my subscription?” (edited 1 Dec 2025, read 21 Nov 2026):

  • Bought on the Headspace website: log in from a desktop or mobile browser (not the mobile app) at headspace.com, visit headspace.com/subscription/manage and tap Cancel Membership. If the button is not there, Headspace asks you to email its support team (the address is on the help page).
  • Bought through Apple: open Settings, tap your name › Subscriptions, select Headspace, tap Cancel Subscription. It stops auto-renewing at the end of the current billing cycle.
  • Bought through Google Play: open the Google Play Store, tap Menu › Subscriptions, tap the subscription, tap Cancel subscription and follow the instructions.

Refunds

Headspace's page says website subscriptions are non-refundable, though it will review special circumstances if you contact support, and that Apple and Google Play purchases are subject to those stores' own refund policies. For an Apple refund, use Apple's “Report a Problem” page; for Google Play, use Google's refund request.

Family plans

Headspace's help centre says only the Family Plan's primary account holder can cancel it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel Headspace in the app?

For a website subscription, Headspace says to use a browser, not the app. For Apple or Google Play, cancel in that store.

Will Headspace refund me?

Headspace says website subscriptions are non-refundable but special cases can be reviewed; store purchases follow Apple's or Google's policy.

Does deleting Headspace cancel it?

No. Cancel where you paid.
