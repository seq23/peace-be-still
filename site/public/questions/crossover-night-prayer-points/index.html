Crossover night prayer points for 31 December

Crossover night is the service many African churches hold on 31 December to pray out the old year and pray in the new one at midnight. Its prayer points usually move in three steps: thanksgiving for the year that is ending, letting go of what is past, and committing the new year into God's hands.

Quick answer

Before midnight, thank God for the year by name and release what is past (Isaiah 43:18-19). At midnight, praise. After midnight, commit the new year to him (Psalm 65:11; Proverbs 3:5-6).

What this page is best for: anyone keeping crossover night at church, at home, or on a call with family in another time zone.

Before midnight: thanksgiving

  1. Thank God for every month of this year: what he gave, and what he kept you from.

    It is of the LORD’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.

    Lamentations 3:22-23 · KJV

  2. Thank him for the people he kept alive, and remember those you lost with gratitude.

    Bless the LORD, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits:

    Psalm 103:2 · KJV

Before midnight: letting go

  1. Lay down the disappointments of the year, and refuse to carry them into the new one.

    Remember ye not the former things, neither consider the things of old. Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth; shall ye not know it? I will even make a way in the wilderness, and rivers in the desert.

    Isaiah 43:18-19 · KJV

  2. Forgive, so you cross over free.

    And when ye stand praying, forgive, if ye have ought against any: that your Father also which is in heaven may forgive you your trespasses.

    Mark 11:25 · KJV

At midnight: praise

Many congregations count down and then break into praise. At home, stand together and sing.

Thou crownest the year with thy goodness; and thy paths drop fatness.

Psalm 65:11 · KJV

After midnight: the new year

  1. Commit the new year, month by month, into God's hands.

    Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.

    Proverbs 3:5-6 · KJV

  2. Ask for wisdom to number your days.

    So teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.

    Psalm 90:12 · KJV

  3. Pray for your household's going out and coming in all year.

    The LORD shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth, and even for evermore.

    Psalm 121:8 · KJV

  4. Pray for the church and the nations in the new year.

    I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men; For kings, and for all that are in authority; that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty.

    1 Timothy 2:1-2 · KJV

Frequently asked questions

Is crossover night the same as Watch Night?

They are close cousins. Watch Night in Black American churches is tied to 31 December 1862 and the Emancipation Proclamation; crossover night is how many African churches name the same midnight service of prayer.

What if my family back home crosses over hours earlier?

Join their midnight on a call, then keep your own. Many families pray twice that night, once in Lagos or Accra time and once in local time.

Which psalm is read at crossover?

Practices differ. Psalm 65 (Thou crownest the year with thy goodness), Psalm 90 and Psalm 121 are common choices.
