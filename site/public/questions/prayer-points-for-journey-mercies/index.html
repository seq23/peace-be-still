Prayer points for journey mercies

Journey mercies are what believers ask God for before travelling: protection on the way, peace while away, and a safe return. Prayer points for journey mercies usually cover three stages: before you leave, on the road or in the air, and when you arrive, with Psalm 121 as the anchor.

Quick answer

Read Psalm 121 together before you leave, commit the travellers and the house left behind, and give thanks when you land. Psalm 121:8 is the verse most families say at the door.

What this page is best for: families flying home to Lagos, Accra, Abuja, Kumasi or anywhere for Christmas, a wedding or a burial, and the people praying for them.

Before you leave

  1. Commit every traveller by name, and the journey from door to door.

    The LORD shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth, and even for evermore.

    Psalm 121:8 · KJV

  2. Ask God to keep the house and the people staying behind.

    The LORD shall preserve thee from all evil: he shall preserve thy soul.

    Psalm 121:7 · KJV

On the road or in the air

  1. Ask for his presence at every airport, connection and checkpoint.

    And, behold, I am with thee, and will keep thee in all places whither thou goest, and will bring thee again into this land; for I will not leave thee, until I have done that which I have spoken to thee of.

    Genesis 28:15 · KJV

  2. Pray for the pilots, drivers and everyone responsible for your safety.

    For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.

    Psalm 91:11 · KJV

  3. Pray for calm when there are delays.

    Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.

    Isaiah 26:3 · KJV

When you arrive

  1. Give thanks as soon as you land, and call home.

    Then are they glad because they be quiet; so he bringeth them unto their desired haven.

    Psalm 107:30 · KJV

  2. Pray for the time with family: kindness, patience and wisdom in every conversation.

    Be kindly affectioned one to another with brotherly love; in honour preferring one another;

    Romans 12:10 · KJV

Before the return journey

Pray the same points again, and add thanksgiving for the time together.

Blessed shalt thou be when thou comest in, and blessed shalt thou be when thou goest out.

Deuteronomy 28:6 · KJV

What else do people ask?

What does “journey mercies” mean?

It is a prayer phrase for God's protection and kindness on a journey, used widely in African and Black American churches.

Which Bible verse is for journey mercies?

Psalm 121 is the most used, especially verse 8. Genesis 28:15, Psalm 91:11 and Deuteronomy 28:6 are also common.

Should I still pray if the flight is short?

Yes. Commit every journey, long or short, to God; Psalm 121 makes no distinction.
