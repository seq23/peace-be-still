What App Store reviewers report (Nov 2026)

Among Hallow's 500 most recent US App Store reviews (24 Mar to 18 Nov 2026), 370 gave 4 or 5 stars and 103 gave 1 or 2. About half of those 103 (51) mention cancelling, refunds or being charged, most often a charge after a trial, or after the reviewer believed they had cancelled. Some low ratings object to paying for prayer at all.

Is it worth paying for a prayer app?

It is worth it if you use it most days and the free part does not cover what you need. Use the free part for two weeks first. Then ask: did I open it daily, and would I pay for a book or a class that did the same? Hallow is a Catholic app with thousands of guided sessions; if that fits your church, its free prayers are a good test.
