Bible verses for anxiety, grouped by what you are feeling

The Bible verses most turned to for anxiety are Philippians 4:6-7, 1 Peter 5:7, Isaiah 41:10, Matthew 6:34 and John 14:27. Philippians 4:6 is the clearest: Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. In 1611 English, “careful” means anxious.

Quick answer

Pick the group below that matches what you feel right now, read one verse slowly, and pray it back in your own words. One verse held for a few days does more than a list read once.

What this page is best for: someone anxious right now who wants a few verses that fit, not fifty.

What does the Bible say about anxiety?

It says to bring it to God rather than carry it alone, and it promises his peace, not the absence of every hard thing. Paul wrote this from prison:

Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

Philippians 4:6-7 · KJV

Note the word “careful”. In the King James English of 1611 it means full of care, worried. The verse is not telling you to be careless; it is telling you not to be eaten up with worry.

Verses for when you are worried about tomorrow

Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself. Sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof.

Matthew 6:34 · KJV

Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.

1 Peter 5:7 · KJV

Verses for when you are afraid

Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.

Isaiah 41:10 · KJV

What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee.

Psalm 56:3 · KJV

What is the most calming verse in the Bible?

There is no official answer, but many people name Jesus's words to his friends the night before the cross:

Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid.

John 14:27 · KJV

Another short one to say under your breath is Be still, and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the heathen, I will be exalted in the earth.

Verses for a racing mind

Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.

Isaiah 26:3 · KJV

In the multitude of my thoughts within me thy comforts delight my soul.

Psalm 94:19 · KJV

How do I pray when I am anxious?

  1. Say plainly what you are anxious about. God already knows; saying it helps you.
  2. Thank him for one true thing, as Philippians 4:6 asks (with thanksgiving).
  3. Ask for what you need today, not for the whole future.
  4. Read one verse from above, slowly, twice.
  5. If it is night, see verses for sleep and anxiety.

When to get more help

Scripture and prayer are a comfort, not a replacement for care. If anxiety is stopping you from working, eating or sleeping for weeks, talk to a doctor or counselor. If you are thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline in the US) now.

Frequently asked questions

Is anxiety a sin in the Bible?

The Bible does not call feeling anxious a sin. It invites anxious people to bring their worry to God: Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you. (1 Peter 5:7). Feeling afraid and praying about it is what the Psalms are full of.

Is there a verse for worry at night?

Psalm 4:8: I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety. Psalm 94:19 also fits a racing mind: In the multitude of my thoughts within me thy comforts delight my soul.

What does “Be careful for nothing” mean?

In the King James Version, “careful” means anxious or full of care. Philippians 4:6 is saying: do not be anxious about anything, but pray about everything, with thanksgiving.

Can a Bible verse take anxiety away?

This page makes no promise like that. Many believers find comfort in holding one verse through a hard season. If anxiety is heavy or lasts, see a doctor or counselor too.
