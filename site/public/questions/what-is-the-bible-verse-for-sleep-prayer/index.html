What is a Bible verse for sleep prayer?

A Bible verse for sleep prayer is a passage of scripture used to center your heart on God before rest. Many find peace in reading or listening to the Psalms. This nightly practice helps quiet the mind by focusing on the word of God rather than the worries of the day.

Published 21 November 2026 · Peace Be Still

Which verses help with rest?

Many people turn to the Psalms when they seek quiet at the end of the day. The words of David offer a way to speak to God when the house is still. You might read Psalm 4:8, which says: 'I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, Lord, only makest me dwell in safety.' This verse reminds us that our safety rests in the hands of the Creator. It is a simple, honest prayer for a tired soul.

Another passage often used for sleep is Psalm 121:4: 'Behold, he that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep.' Knowing that God is always watching provides a sense of comfort. You do not need to carry the weight of the world through the night. You can set your burdens down. Reading these words slowly allows them to sink deep into your spirit. It is not about rushing through a list of verses. It is about dwelling in the truth of the text until your heart feels settled.

  • Psalm 4:8: I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep.
  • Psalm 121:4: He that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep.
  • Psalm 3:5: I laid me down and slept; I awaked; for the Lord sustained me.
  • Psalm 91:1: He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.

How to build a nightly practice

A nightly practice is a rhythm of turning your thoughts toward scripture. You might start by reading a short passage. Then, you can sit in the silence for a few moments. Our app provides whispered readings of the Psalms to help you focus. These recordings are designed to be heard in the quiet of your bedroom. You can use the sleep timer to let the reading fade away as you drift toward rest.

Consistency matters more than the length of your reading. Even five minutes of focused time can change the tone of your evening. You may choose to read the same verse for a week. Let the words become familiar. When you hear them whispered, you can let your guard down. You are not performing a task. You are simply being present with the word of God. This is the foundation of our approach to evening rest.

What is the best Bible verse to read before going to sleep?

There is no single best verse, as every person finds comfort in different passages. Many readers prefer Psalm 4:8 for its direct mention of lying down in peace. You should choose a verse that speaks to your current need and read it slowly until you feel a sense of calm in your heart.
