Is Psalm 23 a Good Choice for Sleep Meditation?

Psalm 23 is a nightly practice of reflecting on the shepherd’s care. It offers a steady rhythm for the end of your day. By listening to the words of David, you turn your thoughts toward the quiet pastures and still waters mentioned in the text, grounding your heart in scripture before you rest.

Published 21 November 2026 · Peace Be Still

Why choose the Psalms for your evening rest?

The Psalms have served as a companion for the weary for generations. When the house grows quiet and the day’s work is done, many find comfort in the familiar cadence of these ancient songs. Psalm 23, in particular, provides a steady focus. It reminds the reader that they are not alone. It speaks of a shepherd who leads, restores, and provides. This is not about a quick fix or a change in mindset. It is simply a way to fill your mind with the word of God as you settle into your bed.

Our app offers a space for this nightly practice. You can listen to whispered readings that keep the tone gentle and unhurried. There are no sudden noises or distracting advertisements here. You are free to focus on the text itself. Whether you choose to read the King James Version or listen to the Reina-Valera 1909 in Mi Refugio, the goal remains the same: to keep the word of God near you until you drift off.
