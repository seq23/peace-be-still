How to cancel a Calm subscription, step by step

To cancel Calm, cancel where you paid: on Calm.com if you subscribed on the website, in your iPhone or iPad Settings if you paid through Apple, or in the Google Play Store if you paid on Android. Removing Calm from your phone does not cancel the subscription.

Quick answer

Website: calm.com/login › profile icon › Settings › Manage Subscription › Cancel Subscription. iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Calm › Cancel Subscription. Android: Google Play › profile icon › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Calm › Cancel Subscription.

What this page is best for: anyone who wants to stop the next Calm charge.

First, where did you pay?

Look at the receipt email or card statement. “Apple” or “APPLE.COM/BILL” means Apple; “Google” means Google Play; “Calm” means the website. Calm's help page says Calm cannot access or cancel subscriptions made through Apple, so an Apple subscription has to be cancelled with Apple.

Calm's steps for each

From Calm's help page, “How to cancel your subscription” (edited 28 Oct 2026, read 21 Nov 2026):

  • Calm.com: log in at calm.com/login in a web browser, click your profile icon (top right), choose Settings › Manage Subscription, click Cancel Subscription and follow the prompts.
  • Apple: open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name › Subscriptions, choose Calm › Cancel Subscription. You can also open Calm and tap Manage Subscription to reach the same Apple page.
  • Google Play: open the Google Play app, tap your profile icon › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, choose Calm › Cancel Subscription.

Calm says that after you cancel, the subscription does not renew and you keep Premium access until the end of the current billing period.

How to check it worked

On iPhone, a cancelled subscription shows an expiry date instead of a Cancel button. On Google Play, it shows as cancelled in Subscriptions. Keep the confirmation email if you get one.

Frequently asked questions

Does deleting Calm cancel my subscription?

No. Cancel where you paid: Calm.com, Apple or Google Play.

Will I lose Calm straight away if I cancel?

Calm says you keep Premium access until the end of the current billing period.

Can Calm cancel an Apple subscription for me?

No. Calm's help page says it cannot access or cancel subscriptions made through Apple.
