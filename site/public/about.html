About Peace Be Still

Peace Be Still is a phone app for the end of the day, made by Spry Labs. It is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

Why the name

“Peace, be still” is what Jesus said to the storm in Mark 4:39, “and the wind ceased, and there was a great calm.” The app is built around that picture: a storm settling into still water.

The rules it keeps

  • Scripture word for word. English verses come from the King James Version and Spanish verses from the Reina-Valera 1909, both public domain in the United States, always with book, chapter and verse. The AI never writes a verse itself.
  • AI is labelled. Every chat answer says it is AI. It is not a pastor or a person, and it never speaks as God.
  • Honest prices. A free lane that stays free, a paid lane at $7.99 a month or $39.99 a year, no trial, no weekly plan, no ads, cancel in two taps.
  • No promises of results. Peace Be Still offers a nightly practice. It is not a treatment, and it promises no outcome.
  • Two doors that keep to themselves. The God's Word door never shows content from the Mindset door.

Who makes it

Peace Be Still is a Spry Labs app. Spry Labs builds small, calm apps and websites with no paid advertising.
