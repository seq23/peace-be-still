Help and support

Something not working, a question about your subscription, or a mistake in the app? Write to us and a person will read it.

If you need help right now

Peace Be Still is not a crisis service, and its chat cannot call anyone for you. If you are in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, reach a person now:

  • Call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (United States). For Spanish, press 2 on a call, or text AYUDA to 988.
  • Call 911 if you or someone near you is in danger now.
  • Abuse of a child or teen: call or text the Childhelp hotline, 1-800-422-4453.
  • Abuse at home: call the National Domestic Violence Hotline, 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788.
  • Outside the United States, call your local emergency number, or find a free line at findahelpline.com.

Write to us

Email info@peacebestillapp.com. Tell us what you were doing and what happened; your iPhone model helps too. We answer by email.

Please leave out anything private that we do not need, such as other people’s names.

Cancel a subscription

Apple runs every Peace Be Still subscription, so you cancel it with Apple, not in the app. On your iPhone:

  • Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Tap Peace Be Still, then Cancel Subscription.

Or follow Apple’s guide to canceling a subscription. Your subscription stays active until the end of the time you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Everything free stays free after you cancel.

Refunds are handled by Apple too: ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore purchases

On a new iPhone, or after you reinstall the app, open Peace Be Still, tap Settings inside the app, then The paid lane › Restore Purchases. Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account (Apple ID) you paid with. If the app says it found nothing to restore, write to us with the date of your Apple receipt.

If you are 13 to 17 and a parent approves purchases with Ask to Buy, your subscription turns on once they approve it. Open the app, or tap Restore Purchases, to see it.

How Peace Be Still uses AI

  • Chat answers are written by AI and are marked as AI in the app. They come from free AI providers, which may keep what you type and learn from it. Leave out names and anything you would not want stored.
  • Scripture is never written by the AI. The AI points to a book, chapter and verse, and the app shows the stored King James Version (English) or Reina-Valera 1909 (Spanish) text word for word.
  • The voices that read aloud are AI voices, not recordings of a person.
  • AI can be wrong. It is not a pastor, a counselor or a doctor. If an answer is wrong or hurtful, tap Report this answer under it, or write to us.

Your data

Your saved verses, journal and settings stay on your phone. If you subscribe, you can delete what the chat remembers about you, and the sessions the app made for you, in the app’s Settings at any time. To ask what we keep about you from the app, or to have it deleted, write to info@peacebestillapp.com.
