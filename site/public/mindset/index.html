Mindset: daily words, calm nights, and a chat that is yours

With a paid plan: unlimited chat, kept private. Your private chats are never used to train AI. Private for up to 75 messages a day. After that you can choose free chat, which isn't private; we always ask first.

Mindset is one of the two doors in Peace Be Still, for calm words in the morning and imagination at night, with no religious content. It has three parts: Morning Word · Daily Declarations, Imagination Nights, and a Mindset chat.

Quick answer

Morning Word gives you one line to say out loud, morning and night. Imagination Nights are 30 whispered sleep sessions built around a scene. The chat talks things through calmly; with a paid plan it is private.

What this page is best for: people who want affirmations, visualization and a private place to think, in one calm app.

Morning Word · Daily Declarations

One short line to say out loud each morning, and again at night, such as I let my shoulders drop. Nothing needs me to rush this morning. or I speak up once today, in a calm voice, about something that matters to me. The daily line is free. A plan adds topics: Calm, Confidence, Work and money, The people in your life, Home, Beginning again. There is at most one gentle reminder a day, and you can turn it off.

Imagination Nights

Whispered night sessions in four parts: a slow body settle, a short teaching, one scene read softly (“The Key in Your Hand”, “Walking In Like You Belong”, “A Quiet House”), then quiet. The screen stays dark and dims, a sleep timer fades the voice out, and nothing plays next. You can also write your own scene: “Write it as if it is true now.” Your first night is free, then one a week; a plan opens one every night.

The Mindset chat

Say what is on your mind and get a short, calm answer. Every answer is marked AI; it is not a counselor or a person. If you mention self-harm or abuse, it shows the 988 Lifeline and other help straight away.

  • Free chat is one chat a day, up to 5 messages. It uses a free AI service that may keep what you type, so the app asks before your first free chat.
  • With a paid plan, chat is private for up to 75 messages a day and never used to train AI. After that you can choose free chat, which isn't private; we always ask first.

What is free, and what a plan adds

  • Free, for good: the daily declaration, your first Imagination Night then one a week, one chat a day, and a journal that stays on your phone. No ads.
  • A plan ($7.99 a month or $39.99 a year): private chat that remembers what you share, a session every night plus ones made for you, declaration topics and offline downloads. No trial; cancel in two taps.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Mindset door religious?

No. The Mindset door has no religious content. It is daily declarations, imagination at night and a calm chat.

Is the Mindset chat private?

With a paid plan, yes: private for up to 75 messages a day and never used to train AI. Free chat uses a free AI service that may keep what you type, and the app asks before your first one.

Is the chat a therapist?

No. It is AI, it says so on every answer, and it is not a counselor or a person. If you mention self-harm, it shows the 988 Lifeline straight away.

Can teenagers use it?

Chat is for ages 13 and up. Under 13, the app sends no chat to our service.
