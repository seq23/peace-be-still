Is there a free trial?

No. Peace Be Still has no trial at all, so nothing turns into a charge by surprise. The free version is not a trial: it does not end. A paid plan starts only when you choose it, with the price in dollars shown before you tap.

Are there ads in the free version?

No. There are no ads in Peace Be Still, free or paid, ever.

What should a free Bible chat never do?

  • Make up a verse. In Peace Be Still the AI only names the reference; the app pastes the stored King James text.
  • Pretend to be a pastor. Every answer is labelled as AI.
  • Promise you money, healing or a result. A Bible chat can pray with you and point you to scripture; it cannot promise outcomes.
  • Hide where your words go. Before your first chat, the app says what you type goes to free AI providers that may learn from it, and to leave out names.
