Black Bible chat: a Bible chat in a Black church voice

A Black Bible chat is a Bible chat app that talks the way the Black church talks: warm, plain, with the cadence of a Sunday sermon and the wisdom of the church mothers, and with verses quoted exactly. In Peace Be Still, that is the journey called Hush Harbor · Black Bible Chat & Stories, on the App Store and Google Play.

Quick answer

Hush Harbor is a chat in a Black church voice that answers with King James verses pasted word for word from a stored copy, plus a daily Bible story told in that voice. The free version includes one chat a day of up to 5 messages and today's story.

What this page is best for: people searching for a Black Bible chat, a Black version of Bible chat, or a Bible app that sounds like their church.

What makes a Bible chat sound like the Black church?

It is the voice more than the words. A sermon rhythm that builds and lands. A church mother's saying used once, in the right place. The old spirituals named, like “Go Down, Moses” and “Wade in the Water”. Plain grammar, never put-on spellings or slang, and never a caricature.

Hush Harbor is named for the hidden places in the woods where enslaved people gathered at night to pray and sing. The name honours that history; the chat itself stays humble about it.

What is inside Hush Harbor?

  • A Bible chat in a Black church voice. Tell it what is on your heart; it answers warmly and points you to scripture. Every answer is labelled as AI.
  • Bible stories told in a Black church voice. A daily story from the core library (Jesus calms the storm, Daniel in the lions' den, Ruth and Naomi and more) told in the Hush Harbor voice, plus Black favorites such as Hagar, the Ethiopian on the desert road, Ebed-melech and Simon of Cyrene.
  • Call and response. Each story has one “say it with me” moment, like “God is good” and “all the time”, with a pause for you to answer.
  • The King James Version, word for word. The AI never writes a verse from memory. It names the reference; the app pastes the stored King James text.

Is the Black Bible chat a real pastor?

No. It is an AI helper, and it says so on every answer. It is not a pastor, a prophet or a person, and it never speaks as God or Jesus. Before your first free chat, the app tells you that what you type goes to free AI providers that may learn from it, so leave out names. If you mention self-harm or abuse, the app does not send it to the AI; it shows the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and other help straight away.

What does it cost?

The free version stays free: the whole King James Bible to read, and nearly every chapter read aloud, today's story, one chat a day of up to 5 messages, and a daily declaration. The paid plan is $7.99 a month or $39.99 a year and adds unlimited chat, chat that remembers what you are carrying, and every past story. With a paid plan: unlimited Bible chat. Private for your first 50 messages each day; after that, chat keeps going, just not private. Your private chats are never used to train AI. There is no trial, no weekly plan and no ads, and you can cancel any time from the paid screen.

Is the black version of Bible chat a different Bible?

No. The Bible is the same in every journey: the King James Version in English, and the Reina-Valera 1909 in Spanish. What changes is the voice of the chat and of the stories. The words of scripture are never changed.
