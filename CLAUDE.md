# peace-be-still

Read first: `README.md` (how the site is built and deployed) and `DESIGN.md` (the approved brand).

- **Paid AI only where she ruled it, capped** (owner rulings 21 Nov 2026): paid chat and the crisis lane use the billed key (`PBS_GEMINI_API_KEY` or the private route in `PAID_PROVIDER`); Made for you drafts and judges on free models first and falls back to the private paid model (DeepSeek v4.1 Flash on OpenRouter zero-retention, never DeepSeek's own API) when they are busy, empty or rate-limited, or when a free draft still misses the checks after one revision (a free model never gets a third try on a note). All of it is capped at $100 a month app-wide (`PAID_AI_MONTHLY_CAP_USD`); at the cap the paid route is skipped. The free lane never reads the billed key; everything else is nothing paid: the website makes no model call and search-loop drafting stays $0 (`config/llm_routing.json`: `max_cost_usd: 0`, no paid fallback, free models only). See `docs/cost/zero-paid-llm.md` and `docs/OPERATIONS.md`; `site/test/cost-rule.test.mjs` reads all three.
- Scripture is quoted word for word: KJV in English, Reina-Valera 1909 in Spanish, 개역한글 (1961) on the Korean pages, the 1919 和合本 (Simplified) on the Chinese pages, always with book, chapter and verse.
- Never the phrase "Black Bible Stories" (registered mark; say "Bible stories told in a Black church voice"; the stories are text and audio, with no pictures, so never claim faces or illustrations), never "Speak Life", manifesting and law-of-attraction words only on Mindset-door pages (never where the faith door sees; `site/test/mindset.test.mjs`), no outcome or health promises, no launch date.
- The God's Word door and the Mindset door never link each other (`site/scripts/check-site.mjs` enforces it). Mindset pages live in `site/content/mindset.mjs`; the home pages in `site/content/home.mjs` (two equal door cards; counts and prices from `facts()` in `parts.mjs`).
- The waitlist never sends email; its only use is one launch email, then the list is deleted.
- **Encrypted files** (public repo): the chat prompts, crisis lists, test question sets and the worker tests are git-crypt ciphertext (`.gitattributes`). Unlock a clone once (covers every worktree): `cd ~/GitHub/boss-os && npm run -s vault:run -- bash <repo>/scripts/git-crypt-unlock.sh`; never `git-crypt unlock` itself. Any new file with prompt or crisis text goes under an encrypted path (`scripts/check-encrypted.mjs` fails on a plain copy).
- Work in your own worktree; run `node site/scripts/check-site.mjs && node --test site/test/*.test.mjs` before pushing.
