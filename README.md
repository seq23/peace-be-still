# Peace Be Still

*A great calm, every night.* A Spry Labs phone app with two doors, God's Word and Mindset. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

This repo holds:

- `site/`: the website at https://peacebestillapp.com (English at `/`, Spanish at `/es/`, a Korean dawn-prayer landing at `/ko/` with its question pages under `/ko/questions/`, and the African diaspora landing at `/prayer-points/`). Plain HTML and CSS served by a Cloudflare Worker with static assets (`site/src/worker.js` only redirects www to the bare domain and adds security headers).
- `search-loop.config.json` and `.github/workflows/search-loop.yml`: the shared search engine (seq23/search-loop) adds faith and sleep question pages under `/questions/`, each ending in the launch waitlist.
- `.github/workflows/threads-posts.yml`: the daily Threads post on @peacebestillapp at 9:30 PM CT through Peace Be Still's own free Buffer (secret `BUFFER_PEACEBESTILL_ACCESS_TOKEN`), from the 60-post bank `data/threads/posts.json`, 4 evenings queued ahead, recycling after 60+ days; ledger `data/threads/posted.json`. Logic and stops: `scripts/threads/threads_loop.mjs`.
- `.github/workflows/instagram-posts.yml`: picture posts Sun, Tue and Thu at 8:00 PM CT, the same pictures and caption to Instagram @peacebestillapp and the Facebook Page "Peace Be Still", same Buffer, from the 30-post bank `data/instagram/posts.json` (pictures in `site/public/social/ig/`, re-rendered with `npm run ig:render` after any post or store change), 3 slots queued ahead per channel, recycling after 60+ days; ledger `data/instagram/posted.json`; writes `data/social/metrics.json` for the Boss OS scorecard row `pbs-social`. Logic and stops: `scripts/instagram/ig_loop.mjs`.
- **Buffer:** the free plan holds 10 scheduled posts: Threads keeps up to 4, Instagram + Facebook up to 6 (`ORG_SPLIT`). Moving to another Buffer is one switch: `TOKEN_ENV` in `scripts/threads/buffer.mjs` plus the `secrets.` line in both workflows (the Instagram validator fails if they disagree). No Buffer organisation or channel id is kept in this repo; channels are matched by service and exact name.
- The app (Expo) arrives in phase 1.

## Website

- **Deploy:** every push to `main` that touches `site/` runs `.github/workflows/site.yml`: the safety check, then `wrangler deploy` from `site/`, then a smoke test of the live pages and the www redirect. Never deploy by hand.
- **Check locally:** `node site/scripts/check-site.mjs && node --test site/test/*.test.mjs`. Preview: `cd site && npx wrangler@4.76.0 dev`.
- **Pages:** the home pages are built from `site/content/home.mjs` and the Mindset hub and pages from `site/content/mindset.mjs`; seed question pages live in `site/content/questions.mjs` (the 21 Nov 2026 diaspora pages in `site/content/diaspora-ko.mjs` and `diaspora-africa.mjs`; Korean pages quote the KJV in English with Korean book names, since no public-domain Korean text is stored) (the 18 Nov 2026 search pages in `site/content/blitz-*.mjs`; scripture only via `site/content/scripture.mjs`); the header, footer and waitlist parts in `site/content/parts.mjs`. After editing either, run `node site/scripts/build-seed-pages.mjs && node site/scripts/build-sitemap.mjs`.
- **Legal pages:** `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/disclaimer` and `/es/privacidad`, `/es/terminos`, `/es/aviso` are built from `legal/*.md` (English, which controls) and `legal/es/*.md` by `site/content/legal.mjs` inside `build-seed-pages.mjs`; never edit their HTML. A change to an English text changes its Spanish twin in the same PR, and a fresh model re-checks the pair side by side (owner ruling 6 Nov 2026: no machine-translated legal text). The es-MX store listing links the Spanish pages only when `es_listing_spanish_legal` in `site/config/store.json` is true (`npm run store:set -- --es-legal on`, owner's yes only).
- **Waitlist:** the form posts to `/api/waitlist` (`site/src/worker.js`) and stores a row in the D1 database `peace-be-still`, table `waitlist` (`site/migrations/`). It never sends email. The only use is one launch email, after which the list is deleted (see `/privacy`).
- **Secrets:** `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN` and `CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID` are GitHub repo secrets. Nothing secret lives in this repo.
- **Previews:** every site PR is published to the Pages project `peace-be-still-site-preview` (branch `pr-<n>`) and captured at 390 px light/dark and 1280 px; list routes in the PR body as `Preview routes: /ko/,/prayer-points/`.
- **Social drafts:** `data/drafts/<batch>/` holds post drafts awaiting her yes (no loop reads them); render their pictures with `npm run ig:render -- --bank <posts.json> --out <dir>`.
- **Images:** `site/assets-src/` holds the HTML used to render `og.png`, `og-es.png`, `og-ko.png`, `og-prayer-points.png` and the PNG icons with headless Chrome.
- **Fonts:** Libre Caslon Text, Figtree and Spline Sans Mono, self-hosted from Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

## Validators

| Check | Protects | Runs |
|---|---|---|
| `site/scripts/check-site.mjs` (+ `--self-test`) | No "Black Bible Stories" (registered mark) or "Speak Life"; no outcome/health promises; manifesting and mindset-practice words only on Mindset pages and no links between the two doors; the only form is the waitlist, no email addresses or third-party scripts (the `/privacy` promise); no store badges or links before launch | every PR, before every deploy, and after every search-loop build |
| `site/test/mindset.test.mjs` | The Mindset door: at least 24 English and 8 Spanish Mindset question pages; no faith word on any Mindset page; no manifesting word on a faith page or the home page's God's Word card; the two home door cards have the same shape; every "unlimited … private" line carries "With a paid plan:"; counts and prices only from the app's settings; no outcome, health, trial, urgency or filler wording; each page answers before it names the app; competitor facts sourced and dated; Mindset pages open the Imagination Nights custom product page | every PR and before every deploy |
| `site/test/scripture.test.mjs` | Every verse on every page equals the stored KJV / Reina-Valera 1909 text (`content/bible/`); a verse block with no stored reference fails. Pages quote scripture only through `site/content/scripture.mjs` | every PR and before every deploy |
| `scripts/threads/validate.mjs` | Threads posts: every verse word for word (KJV / RV1909) at queue time, faith only (no Mindset, Imagination Nights, manifesting, law of assumption), "coming soon" until `site/config/store.json` has the App Store link, only ever the @peacebestillapp channel, the loop's named stops | every PR touching the Threads files, and inside every daily run |
| `scripts/instagram/validate.mjs` | Instagram + Facebook picture posts: every verse word for word on the pictures and in the captions, faith only, AI line on every Hush Harbor / voice post and in every week, Young Faith's age line, hashtags (#blackbiblechat first, never #blackbiblestories), "coming soon" until the store link is set, no italic verse words, pictures re-rendered from their post, only the two named channels, the loop's named stops, the metrics file | every PR touching the social files, and inside every daily run |
| `site/test/legal.test.mjs` | The Spanish legal pages say the same as the English, clause by clause: same sections and paragraphs, and in each paragraph the same numbers, named companies/products/places, email addresses and links (parsed from both texts); each Spanish page opens with "the English version controls"; the built pages equal the current Markdown | every PR and before every deploy |
| `site/test/waitlist.test.mjs` | The waitlist endpoint: honeypot, per-IP limit (429), daily ceiling (503), consent required, same-origin only, remove link deletes the row, www redirect | every PR and before every deploy |
